### Description (4000 chars max)

Do you actually know what's in the food you're eating every day?

Scan any barcode and Healthy Choices instantly scores the product from 0–100 — breaking down ingredients, additives, preservatives, and nutritional value into one clear grade. No nutrition degree required.

**SCAN & SCORE IN SECONDS**
Point your camera at any barcode and get an instant health score. See exactly which ingredients triggered the rating — artificial dyes, preservatives, seed oils, ultra-processed additives — explained in plain English, not chemical names.

**INGREDIENT TRANSPARENCY**
Every flagged ingredient tells you *why* it's flagged. Not just a red dot — a real explanation of what it is and why health-conscious shoppers avoid it.

**COMPANY TRANSPARENCY (Pro)**
This is where Healthy Choices goes further than any other food scanner. See who really owns the brand you're buying — the parent company, their annual lobbying spend in Washington, political donation splits, and the issues they've funded. Know what your grocery dollars support before you buy.

**SEARCH ANY PRODUCT (Pro)**
Looking for something specific? Search products by name without scanning. Great for researching before you shop.

**FULL SCAN HISTORY (Pro)**
Your complete scan history stays organized and searchable. Filter by grade, search by name, and track how your choices have improved over time.

**SHARE SCORES (Pro)**
Found something surprising? Share a product's score card with friends and family in one tap.

## HOSTING YOUR PRIVACY POLICY (quick options)

### Option A — Netlify Drop (easiest, 2 minutes, free)
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop in a browser
2. Drag the entire `web/` folder from your project onto the page
3. Netlify gives you a URL like `https://random-name.netlify.app/privacy`
4. Copy that URL into App Store Connect

### Option B — GitHub Pages (free, permanent)
1. Create a GitHub account at github.com if you don't have one
2. Create a new repo called `healthychoices-web`
3. Upload the contents of the `web/` folder
4. Go to Settings → Pages → set source to main branch
5. Your URL will be `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/healthychoices-web/privacy`

### Option C — Buy a domain (~$12/year)
healthychoicesapp.com or similar on Namecheap/GoDaddy
Point it at Netlify or GitHub Pages for a clean professional URL.
