Go to: app.revenuecat.com → Project → API Keys → iOS public SDK key (starts with "appl_").
Replace the placeholder in src/utils/subscription.js.

✅
Sign in with Apple implemented
Required for any app that offers third-party social login. Already built into AuthScreen.

Account deletion option in app
Required since June 2023 for any app with user accounts. "Delete Account" button added to Profile screen.
⚠️
App Store screenshots (manual)
Required sizes: iPhone 6.7" (1290×2796) and 6.5" (1242×2688). Take them from your phone via TestFlight once the crash is fixed, or use a simulator on a Mac.
Minimum: 3 screenshots per device size. Recommended: 5–6 showing Scan, Score, Company, Search, and Profile screens.
⚠️
Privacy nutrition labels in App Store Connect (manual)
Go to App Store Connect → your app → App Privacy. You collect: Email Address (account creation), Purchase History (subscription), and Usage Data (scan history on-device only — select "not linked to user").
⚠️
Age rating questionnaire (manual)
In App Store Connect → your app → App Information → Age Rating. Answer the questionnaire. Healthy Choices should qualify as 4+ (no objectionable content).
⚠️
App Store description & keywords (manual)
In App Store Connect → your app → App Store Listing. Write a compelling short description (170 chars) and full description. Add up to 100 chars of searchable keywords separated by commas (no spaces after commas).

🚀 Before Going Live
❌
RevenueCat products configured in dashboard
In RevenueCat: create an Entitlement called exactly "Healthy Choices Pro", add two products with IDs "monthly" and "yearly", attach both to the entitlement, then create a Default Offering with both packages. These must match your App Store Connect subscription product IDs exactly.
❌
In-app purchase products created in App Store Connect
In App Store Connect → your app → Monetization → Subscriptions. Create a subscription group "Healthy Choices Pro" with two auto-renewable subscriptions: one monthly ($3.99) and one yearly ($27.99). Set the 7-day free trial on the yearly plan.
❌
Support email updated to support@healthychoices.app
The privacy policy currently lists jamesadventuremarketing@gmail.com. Update the privacy policy and App Store Connect to use support@healthychoices.app before submission. Apple prefers a custom domain email.
✅
Camera usage description in Info.plist
NSCameraUsageDescription is set in app.json — Apple requires a clear reason string.
✅
No private APIs or restricted entitlements
App only uses camera, secure storage, and in-app purchases — all standard entitlements.
✅
Restore Purchases available to free users
Apple requires apps with subscriptions to offer a way to restore — implemented in Profile screen.
