## Explicit trap flags (please double-check in review)

1. **Muir Glen is NOT General Mills anymore.** General Mills sold Muir Glen to Violet Foods (Amphora Equity Partners) in a deal announced January 27, 2026 — six months before this research. Despite `general-mills` already existing as a companyId in `companies.js`, assigning it here would be wrong.
2. **Tazo is NOT Unilever anymore.** Unilever divested its entire tea business (ekaterra — Lipton, PG tips, Pukka, T2, Tazo) to CVC Capital Partners; the sale closed July 1, 2022. The resulting entity is now called Lipton Teas and Infusions. Despite `unilever` already existing as a companyId, assigning it here would be wrong.
3. **Crunchmaster's TH Foods changed hands very recently (June 2025)** — Kameda Seika (Japan) bought out Mitsubishi Corporation's remaining 50% stake, making TH Foods a wholly owned Kameda Seika subsidiary. Anything researched from pre-2025 sources would say "50/50 JV with Mitsubishi," which is now stale.
4. **"JUST Egg" naming collision.** There is a **separate, unrelated UK company** also named "Just Egg" (a shell-egg supplier owned by the Pancholi family) that was acquired by Noble Foods (UK) in June 2025. This is **not** the same company as Eat Just, Inc. (San Francisco, maker of the plant-based JUST Egg product in this catalog, barcode 191011001275). Do not let a search result about "Noble Foods acquires Just Egg" get attached to this product — I verified Eat Just, Inc. remains independent/private with no acquisition found.
5. **Davidson's Safest Choice** is a good reminder that ownership chains matter: National Pasteurized Eggs, Inc. → Michael Foods → Post Holdings. The correct companyId is the ultimate parent (`post-holdings`), not an intermediate name that doesn't exist in the DB.
