Products: "Marketside Southwest Chopped Kit" (10.3 oz, distributed by Walmart, use-by 2023-12-27 to 2024-01-14); "Marketside Bacon Ranch Crunch Kit" (9.8 oz, Walmart, use-by 2024-02-03 to 2024-02-19); "Ready Pac Bistro Fresh Mex Chopped Kit" (10.5 oz, distributed by WinCo, use-by 2024-01-18 to 2024-02-18); "Ready Pac Bistro Queso Crunch Salad Kit" (24 oz, distributed by Costco, use-by 2024-01-13 to 2024-02-18). Recalls Four Salad Kits Due to Possible Health Risk From Listeria monocytogenes](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/ready-pac-foods-inc-recalls-four-salad-kits-due-possible-health-risk-listeria-monocytogenes); [Bonduelle Americas — company notice](https://bonduelleamericas.com/blog/2024/02/09/ready-pac-foods-inc-recalls-four-salad-kits-due-to-possible-health-risk-from-listeria-monocytogenes/), dated 2024-02-09; [Hawaii DOH release](https://health.hawaii.gov/news/files/2024/02/News-Release-Department-of-Health-issues-a-recall-alert-for-four-Ready-Pac-Foods-Inc.-salad-kits-due-to-potential-Listeria-monocytogenes-contamination.pdf).
- **2017-02**: Three Ready Pac Foods Inc. establishments — Swedesboro, NJ; Jackson, GA; and headquarters Irwindale, CA (establishment numbers P-27497, P-32081, P-18502B) — recalled approximately 59,225 pounds of "Puro Picante Blazin Hot" chicken salad, produced 2017-01-17 through 2017-02-17 (use-by dates 2017-01-31 through 2017-03-04), for possible Listeria monocytogenes adulteration. ### FDA / FSIS recalls (Bonduelle USA Inc. — distinct frozen-vegetable entity, Brockport, NY)
- **2015-09 (announced 2015-09-01)**: Bonduelle USA Inc. (Brockport, NY) recalled 9,335 cases of frozen corn, sold under multiple private-label/store brand names including Wylwood Super Sweet Whole Kernel Corn, Market Basket Cut Corn, Bountiful Harvest Whole Kernel Cut Corn, and West Creek Frozen Vegetables, after the State of Tennessee's retail product testing returned a positive result for Listeria monocytogenes. Distributed to NY, NJ, PA, VT, MA, IN, OH, KY, MD, VA, NC, FL, MS, LA. No illnesses reported at time of recall. Sources: [CBS News — "Bonduelle USA recalls frozen corn over Listeria scare"](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/frozen-corn-recalled-over-listeria-scare/); [CNN, 2015-09-01](https://www.cnn.com/2015/09/01/health/bonduelle-frozen-corn-recall); [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/health/bonduelle-usa-recalls-frozen-corn-over-possible-listeria-contamination).
