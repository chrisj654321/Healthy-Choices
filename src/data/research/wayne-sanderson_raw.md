## 1. Ownership chain (VERIFY-don't-assume target — findings below)

- Sanderson Farms, Inc. was a publicly traded (NASDAQ: SAFM) poultry company headquartered in Laurel, Mississippi.
- On August 9, 2021, Cargill, Incorporated and Continental Grain Company announced a definitive agreement, via a 50/50-style joint venture entity, to acquire Sanderson Farms for **$203.00 per share in cash**, a total equity value reported as **$4.53 billion**.
  - Source: PRNewswire, "Cargill and Continental Grain Company to Acquire Sanderson Farms for $203 per Share in Cash and Create a Leading U.S. Poultry Company," August 9, 2021. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cargill-and-continental-grain-company-to-acquire-sanderson-farms-for-203-per-share-in-cash-and-create-a-leading-us-poultry-company-301350829.html
  - Corroborating: Cargill's own 2021 announcement page, https://www.cargill.com/2021/cargill-and-continental-grain-company-to-acquire-sanderson-farms (fetch blocked 403 at research time; title/content confirmed via search-result summary only — treat as UNVERIFIED-by-direct-read, though it is the company's own domain).
- The acquisition **closed July 22, 2022**. Sanderson Farms shares stopped trading on NASDAQ that day; the company became privately held.
  - Source: PRNewswire, "Cargill and Continental Grain Complete Acquisition of Sanderson Farms," July 22, 2022 (directly fetched and read in full). https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cargill-and-continental-grain-complete-acquisition-of-sanderson-farms-301591735.html
- On closing, Cargill and Continental Grain **combined Sanderson Farms with Wayne Farms** (an existing Continental Grain subsidiary) to form a new privately held company, **Wayne-Sanderson Farms**, headquartered in **Oakwood, Georgia**.
  - Same PRNewswire source as above.
  - Corroborating (independent, non-company): Agri-Pulse, "Cargill, Continental Grain finish Sanderson Farms merger," July 2022, https://www.agri-pulse.com/articles/18011-cargill-continental-grain-finish-sanderson-farms-merger (fetch blocked 403 at research time, confirmed via search-result summary only); Georgia Farm Bureau, "Wayne-Sanderson Farms merger complete," https://www.gfb.org/news/ag-news/post/wayne-sanderson-farms-merger-complete (not independently fetched, title/summary only).

  - Source: PRNewswire, July 22, 2022 release (above), directly read.

  - Source: PR Newswire, "Wayne-Sanderson Farms Announces Acquisition of Harrison Poultry," July 28, 2025 (directly fetched and read in full). https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wayne-sanderson-farms-announces-acquisition-of-harrison-poultry-302514995.html

### OSHA
- **Wayne Farms (Jack, Alabama plant) — 11 citations, $102,600 proposed penalties.** OSHA cited Wayne Farms for exposing workers to dangerous machinery, fall, and musculoskeletal-disorder hazards (9 serious, 1 repeat, 1 other-than-serious citation). Investigation began after a complaint from the Southern Poverty Law Center; OSHA's findings stated Wayne Farms failed to record employee injuries, failed to refer employees to physicians, and discouraged employees from seeking medical treatment.
  - Source: WATTPoultry, "Wayne Farms cited for 11 safety violations, fined $102,600," https://www.wattagnet.com/broilers-turkeys/article/15514421/wayne-farms-cited-for-11-safety-violations-fined-102600 (search-summary only, not directly fetched — pre-merger, date of underlying OSHA action appears to be ~2014 based on a related dothaneagle.com article headline; STALE, predates 2020, and predates the Sanderson merger).

## 6. Lawsuits — In re Broiler Chicken Antitrust Litigation (MDL, N.D. Illinois) and related

This is actually **at least three distinct sub-tracks of litigation**, each with different plaintiffs, claims, and outcomes for Sanderson Farms specifically. Do not conflate them.

### (a) Broiler Chicken GROWER Antitrust Litigation (wage-fixing / grow-out services, separate MDL/case from the price-fixing one)
- **Sanderson Farms agreed to a $17.75–17.78 million settlement** (exact figure varies slightly by source: $17.75M vs. $17.78M) to resolve claims that it and other processors (Tyson, Pilgrim's Pride, Perdue, Koch, Sanderson as named defendants; Agri Stats, Foster Farms, Mountaire, Wayne Farms, George's, Peco Foods, House of Raeford, Simmons, Keystone, Fieldale, and O.K. Industries named as alleged co-conspirators, not defendants) conspired to suppress compensation paid to broiler chicken growers (independent farmers who raise the birds under contract) between January 27, 2013 and December 31, 2019. Settled without admission of wrongdoing.
  - Source: Top Class Actions, "Broiler chicken grower antitrust $17.75M class action settlement," https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/closed-settlements/broiler-chicken-grower-antitrust-17-75m-class-action-settlement/; Poultry Producer, "Sanderson Farms Agrees To $17.78 Million Settlement In Broiler Grower Price Fixing Suit," https://www.poultryproducer.com/sanderson-farms-agrees-to-17-78-million-settlement-in-broiler-grower-price-fixing-suit/ (both search-summary level, not directly fetched).
  - Note: **Wayne Farms is listed as an alleged co-conspirator, NOT a settling defendant, in this specific grower case** — relevant since Wayne Farms is now Sanderson's sister brand under Wayne-Sanderson Farms.

### (b) Broiler Chicken END-USER CONSUMER class settlements (retail purchasers)
- On December 20, 2021, the court granted final approval of End-User Consumer Class settlements totaling $181 million with six defendants: Tyson Foods, Fieldale Farms, Peco Foods, George's Inc., Pilgrim's Pride Corp., and Mar-Jac. **Sanderson Farms was NOT among these six 2021 settling defendants.**
- On June 30, 2025, the court granted final approval of a further $22.35 million End-User Consumer Class settlement covering ten additional defendants: Claxton, Foster Farms, House of Raeford, Koch Foods, Mountaire, O.K. Foods, Perdue, **Sanderson**, Simmons, and Wayne. **Sanderson Farms' individual dollar contribution within this $22.35M combined figure was NOT disclosed/broken out in sources reviewed.**
  - Source: Class Action.org, "$203M Broiler Chicken Settlements Resolve Antitrust Lawsuit Claiming Buyers Were Overcharged," https://www.classaction.org/news/203m-broiler-chicken-settlements-resolve-antitrust-lawsuit-claiming-buyers-were-overcharged; Cohen Milstein case page, https://www.cohenmilstein.com/case-study/re-broiler-chicken-antitrust-litigation/ (directly fetched — confirmed the $203.35M total End-User Consumer Class recovery figure and the June 30, 2025 approval date and defendant list, but did NOT break out Sanderson's individual share).
- Total End-User Consumer Class recovery across the full litigation: **$203.35 million** (aggregate across all defendants/settlements, not Sanderson-specific).

### (c) Direct Action Plaintiffs (DAP) trial — Walmart, Sysco, and other large direct purchasers
- Sanderson Farms did **NOT settle** with this group of plaintiffs (major direct purchasers including Walmart and Sysco) and instead went to trial.
- **Jury trial, U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Illinois. Six-week trial. Verdict announced October 25–30, 2023 (reported as both dates across sources; treat as late October 2023).** A unanimous jury **rejected the plaintiffs' claims** that Sanderson Farms participated in a conspiracy to inflate broiler chicken prices between 2008 and 2012 via data-sharing (through Agri Stats) and breeder-flock-size reduction. **Sanderson Farms was cleared / found not liable.** Had Sanderson lost, exposure was reported as up to $7 billion in damages (trebled to $21 billion under antitrust law) — this exposure figure is moot given the verdict, included only for context, NOT as an amount owed.
  - Source: Food Dive, "Sanderson Farms cleared in blockbuster price-fixing case," https://www.fooddive.com/news/sanderson-farms-cleared-in-blockbuster-price-fixing-case/698160/ (directly fetched and read in full); corroborating: Bloomberg Law, "Sanderson Farms Cleared of Claims Over Chicken Price-Fixing," https://news.bloomberglaw.com/antitrust/sanderson-farms-cleared-of-chicken-price-fixing-antitrust-claims (title only, not fetched).

**Summary for fact-checker:** Sanderson Farms (a) SETTLED the grower/wage-fixing case for ~$17.75-17.78M, (b) SETTLED as part of a 10-defendant, $22.35M End-User Consumer Class settlement approved June 2025 (individual Sanderson share not disclosed), and (c) WON at trial (cleared of liability) against Direct Action Plaintiffs (Walmart/Sysco-type large buyers) in October 2023. All three are distinct proceedings within/around the broader "In re Broiler Chicken Antitrust Litigation" MDL. No newer post-merger litigation specifically naming "Wayne-Sanderson Farms" as a defendant (as opposed to legacy Sanderson Farms or Wayne Farms individually) was located beyond the NLRB decertification case noted in Section 5.
