## Issues (recalls, litigation)

All entries below are FSIS (USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service) recalls unless noted; recalls are administrative/regulatory actions, not adjudicated liability findings.

1. **May 4, 2021 — undeclared soy allergen / misbranding recall.** Approx. 3,927 lbs of not-ready-to-eat "Michelina's Spaghetti with Meat Sauce," 8.5-oz trays, lot code J1112N8, best-by 22APR2022, UPC 7 17854 10503 9. Cause: soy-containing ingredients may have commingled with product not normally containing soy; soy not declared on label. No confirmed adverse-reaction reports. Source: [FSIS recall notice](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/bellisio-foods-inc--recalls-beef-pasta-products-due-misbranding-and-undeclared); also covered by [MEAT+POULTRY](https://www.meatpoultry.com/articles/24918-misbranding-prompts-bellisio-foods-product-recall), 2021-05-05.

2. **December 2015 — foreign matter (extraneous material) recall.** Approx. 285,264 lbs of boneless pork rib shaped patty frozen entree products recalled for possible contamination with extraneous materials. Source: [FSIS recall notice](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/bellisio-foods-recalls-boneless-pork-rib-frozen-entree-products-due-possible-foreign) (referenced via FSIS establishment recall history).

3. **February 23, 2019 — foreign matter (glass/hard plastic) recall.** Approx. 173,376 lbs of frozen, not-ready-to-eat "Boston Market Home Style Meals Boneless Pork Rib Shaped Patty with BBQ Sauce & Mashed Potatoes," 14-oz black cardboard boxes, produced 12/07/2018–02/15/2019, best-by dates 12/07/2019, 01/04/2020, 01/24/2020, or 02/15/2020. Cause: consumer complaints of glass or hard plastic extraneous material in the rib-shaped patty. Source: [FSIS recall notice](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/bellisio-foods-inc--recalls-boneless-pork-rib-frozen-entree-products-due-possible); also [The National Provisioner](https://www.provisioneronline.com/articles/107470-frozen-pork-entrees-recalled-due-to-foreign-matter-contamination), 2019-02-25.

4. **October 14, 2024 — voluntary recall tied to BrucePac Listeria supply-chain incident.** BrucePac (a third-party supplier) recalled approx. 12 million lbs of ready-to-eat meat/poultry on 2024-10-17 for possible Listeria monocytogenes contamination, discovered via FSIS routine finished-product testing (source: [FSIS BrucePac recall notice](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/brucepac-recalls-ready-eat-meat-and-poultry-products-due-possible-listeria)). Bellisio Foods issued a voluntary recall (announced 2024-10-14 per [Businesswire notice](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241014885322/en/Voluntary-Product-Recall-Notification-Bellisio-Foods-Inc.)) of certain Boston Market, Michelina's, and Atkins products containing chicken sourced from BrucePac's affected facility; Bellisio stated it immediately ceased using ingredients from that facility. Specific recalled items reported: Atkins Chicken & Broccoli Alfredo (code J4281, best-by 10/07/2025); Boston Market Chicken Fajita Rice Bowl (code J4268, best-by 09/24/2025); Michelina's Chicken Fried Rice (codes J4239 and J4270, best-by on/before 08/26/2025 or 09/26/2025). No confirmed adverse-reaction reports per FSIS and company statements. Source: [Today.com recall roundup](https://www.today.com/health/recall/chicken-recall-list-listeria-brucepac-rcna175276); [NBC Chicago](https://www.nbcchicago.com/news/local/list-of-chicken-meat-products-from-grocery-stores-listeria-recall-brucepac/3575732/).
