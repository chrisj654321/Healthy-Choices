## Issues

All items below concern Gorton's, Inc. / Slade Gorton & Co., Inc. (the Gloucester, MA subsidiary) unless noted.

### FDA recalls
- **2007–2008 tampering incident:** FDA found that pills (over-the-counter herbal supplement) discovered in product were "an isolated incident of product tampering that occurred in the consumer's household." Gorton's voluntarily recalled about 1,000 cases of "6 Crispy Battered Fish Fillets" produced October 16, 2007, distributed in 11 states. Source: https://www.seafoodsource.com/news/supply-trade/fda-consumer-tampered-with-gorton-s-fish-fillets (queried 2026-08-23).
- **2022 bone-fragment recall:** Gorton's voluntarily recalled more than 500 packages of "Gorton's Fish Sandwich – 100% Whole Fillets," 18.3 oz, UPC 0-44400-15440-6, date code 2060F2, time range 15:30–17:30, due to potential presence of large and/or sharp bone fragments (choking hazard). Distributed to Hannaford (NY, VT), Giant/Giant Martin's (PA, VA, MD), and other retailers. No injuries reported. Source: FDA — https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/gortons-issues-voluntary-recall-small-quantity-fish-sandwich-fillets (queried 2026-08-23).
- **2026 Listeria recall (Slade Gorton & Co., Inc.):** Recalled one lot (lot 3896) of "Wellsley Farms Farm-Raised Atlantic Salmon," 2-lb bags, UPC 888670025963, production date June 30, 2025, best-by June 30, 2027, sold exclusively at BJ's Wholesale Club in DE, MD, NJ, NY, NC, PA, VA between January 31–February 7, 2026, after FDA random sampling detected potential Listeria monocytogenes. Recall initiated February 10, 2026; FDA assigned Class II risk classification March 4, 2026; recall number H-0541-2026. No illnesses reported. Source: FDA — https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/slade-gorton-co-inc-recalls-one-lot-wellsley-farms-farm-raised-atlantic-salmon-sold-bjs-wholesale (queried 2026-08-23).

### Civil litigation (alleged, not adjudicated)
- **Spindel et al. v. Gorton's Inc.**, Case No. 1:22-cv-10599, U.S. District Court, District of Massachusetts, filed April 21, 2022. Class action alleging Gorton's tilapia products are falsely marketed as "sustainably sourced," alleging fish come from "large industrial fish farms" in China using pond aquaculture with routine antibiotic/biocide treatment, and citing FDA rejection of over 200 tilapia shipments from China (2007–2018) for harmful chemicals/antibiotics. Status: pending (per source, class not yet certified). Source: https://www.classaction.org/news/class-action-alleges-gortons-tilapia-products-sustainability-claims-are-false (queried 2026-08-23). **Allegation only — not adjudicated.**
