## Issues

All items below concern Bar-S Foods Co. (Phoenix, AZ HQ; plants include Altus, OK and Lawton, OK) unless noted.

### USDA FSIS recalls (adjudicated — USDA-confirmed recall action)
- **July 19, 2016:** Bar-S Foods Company (Altus, OK establishment) recalled approximately 372,684 lbs of ready-to-eat chicken and pork hot dog and corn dog products for possible Listeria monocytogenes contamination. Brands affected: Bar-S, Bar-S Extra Lean, Coronado, Chuck Wagon, E-Z Carve, Thrifty. Products produced July 10–13, 2016. FSIS stated: "The firm had not received test results for Listeria monocytogenes in connection with the recalled products, but due to recurring Listeria species issues at the firm, it decided to remove the products from commerce as a precautionary measure." Shipped nationwide. No confirmed illnesses. Source: FSIS — https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/bar-s-foods-company-recalls-chicken-and-pork-hot-dog-and-corn-dog-products-due (queried 2026-08-23).
- FSIS explicitly cites "recurring Listeria species issues at the firm" as context for this recall (i.e., a pattern predating this specific event, per FSIS's own language). Same source as above.

### OSHA (adjudicated — citations issued and closed)
- **Lawton, OK plant (802 SW Goodyear Blvd) — Inspection #314933516:** Opened July 14, 2011 following a fatality on July 12, 2011 — an employee's legs were caught in an auger of a meat processing machine during sanitation while machinery remained operational; employee died after hospitalization. One serious violation cited: Standard 19100147 C04 II (lockout/tagout-related), issued December 8, 2011, penalty $7,000 (unchanged at closure). Case closed January 24, 2012. Source: OSHA — https://www.osha.gov/ords/imis/establishment.inspection_detail?id=314933516 (queried 2026-08-23).
- **Altus, OK plant (500 S Bar S Blvd) — Inspection #1486294.015, "Bar-S Foods Co., A Sigma Company":** Opened July 31, 2020, closed December 2, 2020, classified Fatality/Catastrophe. A 67-year-old male stuffer operator in the bologna department tested positive for COVID-19 on July 13, 2020, was directed to quarantine at home, and died after hospitalization on July 31, 2020. Keyword: "Infectious Disease." No individual citation numbers/penalties listed in the public record retrieved. Source: OSHA — https://www.osha.gov/ords/imis/establishment.inspection_detail?id=1486294.015 (queried 2026-08-23).

### Corporate history
- Sigma Alimentos (via parent Alfa SAB) announced acquisition of Bar-S Foods Co. on August 9, 2010; deal closed September 6, 2010. Bar-S was described as the second-largest national brand in the US packaged-meat sector at the time. Sources describe the closing as "subject to customary regulatory approvals" but do not detail any specific FTC antitrust proceeding. Source: https://www.provisioneronline.com/articles/92600-sigma-alimentos-acquires-bar-s-foods-1; https://www.sigmafoods.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/september6_2010.pdf (queried 2026-08-23). **No FTC action found — inconclusive, not confirmed absent.**
