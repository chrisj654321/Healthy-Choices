## Issues (regulatory/legal — US subsidiaries)

### FDA
- **2002-05-07**: FDA Chicago District Office issued a Warning Letter to the president of Optimum Nutrition, Inc. (Aurora, IL), following a July 25–27, 2001 inspection. Cited violations: (1) Natural Opti-Soy 50, Natural PRO COMPLEX, and Natural 100% Egg Protein products were adulterated for containing stevia, which FDA had not recognized as GRAS (generally recognized as safe) or approved as a food additive at that time; (2) Natural Opti-Soy 50 was misbranded for a label claim that soy protein inhibits cancer/atherosclerosis effects without the product meeting FDA's minimum 6.25g soy protein threshold required to carry that health claim. Source: [Quackwatch — Warning Letter to Optimum Nutrition/Anthony Costello](https://quackwatch.org/cases/fdawarning/prod/fda-warning-letters-about-products-2002/optimum/), letter dated 2002-05-07.
- **2015-09-23 (approx., per Citeline article)**: FDA asserted Optimum Nutrition and Twinlab protein bars were "misbranded." Source: [Citeline/Pink Sheet — "Optimum Nutrition, Twinlab Protein Bars 'Misbranded,' FDA Asserts"](https://insights.citeline.com/PS092315/Optimum-Nutrition-Twinlab-Protein-Bars-Misbranded-FDA-Asserts/) — full article behind paywall; exact citation/date and FDA action type (Warning Letter vs. import alert vs. other) not independently confirmed from the snippet. Flag as **unconfirmed detail, alleged in trade press**, needs a direct read of the article or the underlying FDA document to verify.
- **2022-05-04 Warning Letter — RETRACTED**: FDA issued a May 9, 2022 constituent update naming "Glanbia Performance Nutrition (Manufacturing), Inc." among 11 companies that received Warning Letters (dated 2022-05-04) for illegally selling adulterated dietary supplements (products cited: "Uplift Max" and "Shred Her Max"). FDA subsequently determined Glanbia was misidentified — the offending website had incorrectly claimed to be a property of Optimum Nutrition/Glanbia — and **retracted the Warning Letter against Glanbia**, continuing its investigation to find the actual responsible firm. Sources: [WholeFoods Magazine — "FDA Retracts Warning Letter to Glanbia Performance Nutrition"](https://www.wholefoodsmagazine.com/articles/6238-fda-retracts-warning-letter-to-glanbia-performance-nutrition); [FDA — "FDA Sends Warning Letters to Multiple Companies for Illegally Selling Adulterated Dietary Supplements"](https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-sends-warning-letters-multiple-companies-illegally-selling-adulterated-dietary-supplements), dated 2022-05-09. **This is a confirmed non-adjudication — the warning letter was withdrawn/erroneous, not upheld.**

### Civil litigation (class actions — consumer/labeling; not a regulatory action, included because it names the US entity directly and involves label/marketing claims)
- **John Daly et al. v. Glanbia Performance Nutrition Inc.**, Case No. 2023CH00096, Circuit Court of Cook County, Illinois, Chancery Division. Class action alleging think! protein bars were falsely marketed as containing "no artificial sweeteners" while containing maltitol syrup, a synthetic/sugar alcohol sweetener; plaintiff purchased think! Creamy Peanut Butter bars in December 2022. Status: alleged, not adjudicated (as of source publication). Source: [Top Class Actions — "Think class action claims protein bars falsely advertised as containing no artificial sweeteners"](https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/consumer-products/food/think-class-action-claims-protein-bars-falsely-advertised-as-containing-no-artificial-sweeteners/).
- **Tocci et al. v. The Isopure Company, LLC d/b/a Nature's Best, and General Nutrition Corp.**, Case No. 14-cv-09097, S.D.N.Y. Alleges "protein-spiking" — Isopure "100% Whey Protein Isolate" products advertised at 50g protein/serving allegedly used cheaper non-protein ingredients (added amino acids) to inflate nitrogen-test protein readings. Status: alleged in complaint; outcome not confirmed in this pass (source: [ClassAction.org — "Protein Spiking Lawsuit | Misleading Labels"](https://www.classaction.org/amino-spiking-lawsuit)).

Status: alleged, not adjudicated per source.
