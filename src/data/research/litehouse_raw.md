## Issues

### FDA Warning Letter — MARCS-CMS 662949 (Nov 29, 2023) — ADJUDICATED (government enforcement action)
- FDA issued a Warning Letter to Litehouse, Inc., dated November 29, 2023, reference MARCS-CMS 662949.
- Cited violation: misbranding under section 403(w) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act — failure to declare a major food allergen (soy) on the finished product label. Introducing misbranded food into interstate commerce is prohibited under FD&C Act section 301(a).
- This is a labeling/allergen-declaration violation, not a sanitation citation — no sanitation (21 CFR Part 117 / GMP) violation is stated in the warning letter per FDA's letter summary.
- Facility/product tied to the letter: Simple Truth brand Plant Based Ranch Dressing.
- Linked recall: on April 19, 2023, Litehouse recalled Simple Truth brand Plant Based Ranch Dressing after investigating consumer complaints. Root cause: work-in-progress bulk containers used to produce the Plant Based Ranch Dressing were mislabeled and were actually filled with Caesar Dressing; the Caesar Dressing contains soy, which was not declared on the Plant Based Ranch Dressing label. Affected lot: 1713802052623, "Best if used by 05 23 23."
- Source: FDA Warning Letters database, "litehouse-inc-662949-11292023," https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/litehouse-inc-662949-11292023 (accessed 2026-08-23; direct fetch of the page returned HTTP 404 at time of research, content confirmed via search-engine indexing/summary of the same FDA page).

### Recall — Undeclared anchovies, Brite Harbor Caesar Dressing & Dip (Feb 15, 2021) — FACTUAL (FDA-listed recall)
- Litehouse Inc. voluntarily recalled a limited quantity of Brite Harbor Caesar Dressing & Dip 1.5 oz pillow packets due to possible undeclared anchovies. Announcement/FDA publish date: February 15, 2021.
- Scope: limited to one production day, one manufacturing location; 225 cases of 60/1.5 oz pouches distributed to a limited number of customers in Washington, Oregon, Idaho, and Utah.
- Lot/date code: "Best Used By 03 071321," lot code 03 071321 16002.
- No adverse reaction reports stated in the notice.
- Source: FDA Recalls, Market Withdrawals & Safety Alerts, "Litehouse Inc. ### Civil lawsuit — Cassar v. Litehouse, Inc. (filed Dec 27, 2025) — ALLEGED, pending (not adjudicated)
- Case: Gina Cassar v. Litehouse Inc. Case No. 627818/2025, New York Supreme Court, Nassau County. Filed December 27, 2025.
- Allegation (unproven): Litehouse's Avocado Ranch Dip & Spread is labeled "No Artificial Colors, Flavors or Preservatives" while containing phosphoric acid, which plaintiff alleges is a synthetic, chemically manufactured ingredient that functions as a preservative (lowering pH, inhibiting microbial growth, buffering acidity, extending shelf life, preventing oxidation). Phosphoric acid is disclosed in the ingredient list but not on the front label.
- Plaintiff Gina Cassar alleges she purchased the product in New York between January 2023 and September 2025, paid a price premium (~$4.49), and would not have purchased or would have paid less had she known. Seeks class certification, actual damages, injunctive relief, attorney fees.
- This is an unproven civil complaint — no judgment, settlement, or finding of liability confirmed in this pass.
- Source: Juris Law Group, "Class Actions Lawsuits Newsletter, January 2026," https://jurislawgroup.com/class-actions-lawsuits-newsletter-january-2026/ (accessed 2026-08-23); case number/court corroborated by Law.com Radar docket listing, https://www.law.com/radar/card/newyork-nassau-2776108-gina-cassar-v-litehouse-inc (accessed 2026-08-23).
