## 3. CRIMINAL antitrust conviction — Pilgrim's Pride's own conduct (the big one)

- **Case:** *United States v. Pilgrim's Pride Corporation*, Case No. **1:20-cr-00330-RM**, U.S. District Court for the District of Colorado (Denver).
  - Source: CourtListener docket, https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/18533743/united-states-v-pilgrims-pride-corporation/
  - Source: DOJ Antitrust Division case document (plea agreement/information), https://justice.gov/atr/case-document/file/1330236/dl
- **Charge:** One count of conspiracy in restraint of trade (price-fixing and bid-rigging for broiler chicken products) under 15 U.S.C. § 1 (Sherman Antitrust Act). Conspiracy alleged to run from as early as 2012 through at least 2017 (per DOJ), with the charged offense described as ending January 2019 in the court judgment.
- **Plea agreement announced:** October 13/14, 2020, via Pilgrim's Pride 8-K and press release. Pilgrim's Pride became the **first company** to plead guilty in the DOJ's broiler-chicken antitrust investigation.
  - Source: Pilgrim's Pride 8-K, Oct 14, 2020, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/802481/000080248120000073/ex991-ppcpressrelease1.htm
  - Source: Pilgrim's Pride IR press release, "Pilgrim's Announces Agreement with DOJ Antitrust Division," https://ir.pilgrims.com/news-releases/news-release-details/pilgrims-announces-agreement-doj-antitrust-division
- **Change of plea and sentencing hearing:** **February 23, 2021**, before Judge Raymond P. Moore (U.S. District Court, District of Colorado).
- **Criminal fine:** Court-assessed criminal monetary penalty of **$107,923,572.00** (commonly rounded to "$107 million" or "$107.9 million" in press). Note: the original plea agreement figure announced in Oct 2020 was $110,524,140 — the court's final assessed fine at sentencing was the lower $107,923,572.00 figure.
  - Source: DOJ Office of Public Affairs press release, "One of the Nation's Largest Chicken Producers Pleads Guilty to Price Fixing and is Sentenced to a $107 Million Criminal Fine," https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/one-nation-s-largest-chicken-producers-pleads-guilty-price-fixing-and-sentenced-107-million (direct WebFetch returned HTTP 403 both attempts — content below is from search-engine-indexed snippet of this exact DOJ URL, treat as UNVERIFIED-by-direct-fetch though the URL itself is unambiguously the primary DOJ source page).
  - Source (exact fine figure, sentencing date, judge name): "USA v. Pilgrim Pride Corp — Judgment in a Criminal Case," Feb 23, 2021, hosted by Penn State Ag Law Center, https://aglaw.psu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/USA-v.-Pilgrim-Pride-Corp-Judgment-in-a-criminal-case-2.23.21.pdf — this appears to be a copy of the actual court judgment document (primary-source-equivalent), not independently re-opened by me this pass, but content pulled via search snippet quoting it directly.
- **Scope of affected commerce:** DOJ states the conspiracy affected **at least $361 million** in Pilgrim's own sales of broiler chicken products.
- **First-mover note:** DOJ press materials describe Pilgrim's as the first of the poultry-industry defendants to plead guilty in this investigation; the same DOJ press release states the ongoing investigation had by that point (Feb 2021) yielded charges against 10 individuals.

## 5. Individual executive criminal charges — same broiler-chicken conspiracy (notable: all resolved without conviction)

- **First trial group (indicted 2020, tried multiple times):** Five individuals were criminally charged and tried, including two former Pilgrim's Pride CEOs — **Bill Lovette** (former CEO) and **Jayson Penn** (CEO at time of trial) — plus former Pilgrim's Pride VP of sales **Roger Austin**, and two Claxton Poultry executives, Mikell Fries and Scott Brady (Claxton is a separate company, not a Pilgrim's Pride subsidiary).
- **Outcome:** All five were **acquitted by a jury on July 7, 2022**, in the U.S. District Court in Denver. This was the **DOJ's third trial attempt** in this case (the first two ended in mistrials/hung juries per industry press). Each was charged with a single count of conspiracy to restrain trade, carrying a maximum 10-year sentence.
  - Source: The Poultry Site, "Poultry execs acquitted in US price-fixing case," https://www.thepoultrysite.com/news/2022/07/poultry-execs-acquitted-in-us-price-fixing-case
  - Source: Fox Business, "5 executives from poultry industry found not guilty of price fixing," https://www.foxbusiness.com/features/5-executives-poultry-industry-found-not-guilty-price-fixing
  - Source: Skadden client memo, "Chicken Executives Acquitted in Third Trial. What Next for the DOJ?", https://www.skadden.com/-/media/files/publications/2022/08/chicken_executives_acquitted_in_third_trial_what_next_for_the_doj.pdf
- **Second indictment group (July 2021):** A federal grand jury indicted four additional former/current Pilgrim's Pride executives — **Jason McGuire, Timothy Stiller, Wesley "Scott" Tucker, and Justin Gay** — along with Koch Foods (a separate company) on related price-fixing charges.
  - Source: DOJ Office of Public Affairs, "Four Executives and Company Charged with Price Fixing in Ongoing Investigation of Broiler Chicken Industry," https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/four-executives-and-company-charged-price-fixing-ongoing-investigation-broiler-chicken (July 2021)
- **Outcome:** Charges against all four were **dismissed by DOJ**, not tried: Gay and Tucker's charges dismissed **August 11, 2022**; McGuire and Stiller's charges dismissed **October 17, 2022**.
  - Source: Ground News aggregation citing court filings, https://ground.news/article/four-more-former-pilgrims-pride-execs-indicted — secondary source; exact dismissal dates should be re-confirmed against the D. Colorado docket by fact-check if possible.
- **Net result:** Despite Pilgrim's Pride Corporation itself pleading guilty as a company, **every individual executive criminally charged in connection with the same alleged conspiracy was either acquitted or had charges dismissed** — no individual was convicted.

## 10. EPA / Clean Water Act — Suwannee River, Live Oak, Florida

- Environmental groups (Environment America, Environment Florida, Sierra Club) filed a Clean Water Act citizen suit alleging Pilgrim's Pride's Live Oak, FL plant discharged wastewater into the Suwannee River exceeding permitted pollution limits — cited as **1,377 days of violations since 2012**, with discharges reported as up to triple legal limits, and 379,641 lbs of toxic pollution released into the river in 2014 per EPA Toxics Release Inventory data (as characterized by the plaintiff groups).
- **Settlement (November 2017):** Pilgrim's Pride agreed to pay **$1.43 million total** — **$1.3 million** to fund a "Sustainable Farming Fund" for soil/groundwater/surface-water quality improvement in the Suwannee Basin, and **$130,000** as a civil penalty to the U.S. Treasury. Plaintiff groups characterized this as "believed to be the largest Clean Water Act penalty in a citizen enforcement suit in Florida history" — this is the plaintiff groups' own characterization, not an independently confirmed superlative.
  - Source: Environment America, "Environmental Groups Reach Major Clean Water Settlement with Pilgrim's Pride," https://environmentamerica.org/news/ame/environmental-groups-reach-major-clean-water-settlement-pilgrims-pride — NOTE: this is the plaintiff/advocacy party's own press release, not an independent or court source. The settlement itself (amount, terms) should be treated as more reliable than the "largest in FL history" characterization.
  - Cross-reference: National Environmental Law Center case page, https://www.nelc.org/cases/environment-florida-sierra-club-v-pilgrims-pride-corp/
