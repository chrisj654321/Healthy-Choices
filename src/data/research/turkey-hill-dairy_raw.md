### FDA recall — undeclared allergen
- **Voluntary recall, April 20, 2022.** Turkey Hill Dairy recalled select 48 oz. containers of Chocolate Marshmallow Premium Ice Cream because select containers may have been inadvertently filled with Chocolate Peanut Butter Cup Ice Cream during production, creating an undeclared-peanut allergen risk. Recall covered approximately 385 containers, UPC 020735420935, sell-by date 03/02/2023, sold between April 14–19, 2022. Company stated it had not received reports of illness. (Source: FDA — https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/turkey-hill-dairy-issues-voluntary-recall-and-allergy-alert-undeclared-peanut-select-chocolate; https://www.turkeyhill.com/press/turkey-hill-dairy-press/news-releases/2022-04-20-voluntary-recall; https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/consumer-products/recalls/turkey-hill-recalls-chocolate-marshmallow-ice-cream-over-undeclared-peanuts/)
- This is a **voluntary company-initiated recall with FDA notice posting, not an FDA Warning Letter or enforcement action.** No FDA Warning Letter addressed to Turkey Hill was located in this search.

### Consumer litigation — vanilla labeling
- **Russell Kane and Jane Doe v. Turkey Hill Dairy**, filed in Brooklyn (Kings County) Supreme Court, New York, on or about August 21, 2019. Class-action complaint alleged Turkey Hill's vanilla-labeled ice cream products (named product lines: All Natural Ice Cream, Premium Ice Cream, Light Ice Cream, No Sugar Added Ice Cream, Ice Cream Sandwiches, Sundae Cones, Individual Selections) misrepresented the source and amount/proportion of vanilla flavor and used "Natural Flavors" labeling the plaintiffs alleged was misleading. Damages sought: $5 million. As of the source article (published August 22, 2019), Turkey Hill had not responded to requests for comment and no outcome/settlement was reported. (Source: https://patch.com/us/across-america/ice-cream-lovers-are-suing-turkey-hill-name-vanilla)
- Status: **Alleged only** — no adjudication or settlement outcome located in this search.
