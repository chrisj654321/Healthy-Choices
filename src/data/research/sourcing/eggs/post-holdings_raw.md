## Housing systems
- Egg Beaters: sold in a "Cage-Free" labeled variant (USDA-recognized term) — confirmed via multiple retailer product listings (Kroger, Safeway, Vons, Shaw's) for "Egg Beaters Original Cage-Free Liquid Egg Substitute." Whether ALL Egg Beaters SKUs (vs. only the labeled "Cage-Free" variant) are cage-free-sourced was not confirmed — the existence of a distinct "Cage-Free" labeled SKU implies a non-cage-free (conventional caged) variant also exists under the same brand. — Source: https://www.kroger.com/p/egg-beaters-original-cage-free-liquid-egg-substitute/0019637000051
- Davidson's Safest Choice (produced by National Pasteurized Eggs, acquired by Post/Michael Foods in 2016 — see Enforcement section below): retailer listings show both a "Cage Free" labeled SKU and non-labeled SKUs. A 2016 HSUS/FTC complaint alleged that Davidson's carton packaging depicted "lush open pastures, a red barn and free-roaming hens" while (per the complaint's allegation) at least some of the eggs pictured this way came from hens in battery cages — see Enforcement section for full detail; this is a disputed/alleged claim, not an adjudicated fact. — Source: https://www.foodsafetynews.com/2016/10/humane-society-says-egg-carton-artwork-is-false-advertising/
- Michael Foods (parent egg operation): Post Holdings announced (undated press release located via search, project described as running "spring 2017 through 2020") that its Michael Foods business would convert its egg-laying farm in Bloomfield, Nebraska from conventional housing to cage-free, replacing 32 layer barns with 12 cage-free houses. — Source: https://www.einpresswire.com/article/342240830/post-holdings-converting-michael-foods-bloomfield-nebraska-egg-farm-to-cage-free-housing
- Company-authored (not third-party verified) animal welfare statement: Post Holdings' sustainability webpage states general principles ("provide all animals with living environments and nutrition that are in line with science-based industry standards") and describes an internal Animal Welfare Committee with named external advisors (Drs. Johnson, Swanson, and Croney), "routine internal and third-party audits," and a commitment to report progress "at least annually." The page does not name Certified Humane, GAP, or AWA as certifications Post holds — the only named program is "Protein PACT" (an industry collective-action initiative, not a housing/welfare certification), and that is attributed specifically to Bob Evans Farms' participation, not to the egg business generally. — Source: https://www.postholdings.com/home/sustainability/sourcing/animal-welfare/

## Enforcement / litigation

1. **In re Processed Egg Products Antitrust Litigation** (egg-industry price-fixing antitrust MDL)
   - Case: No. 08-md-02002, U.S. District Court, Eastern District of Pennsylvania.
   - Michael Foods, Inc. was named as a DEFENDANT (not plaintiff). Plaintiffs were direct purchasers of shell eggs/egg products, including named plaintiffs Kraft Foods Inc., General Mills Inc., Kellogg Co., and Nestlé S.A. The suit alleged that Michael Foods, along with roughly 10 other egg producers and the United Egg Producers (UEP) trade group, conspired to restrict egg supply and control prices in violation of federal antitrust law.
   - Status: SETTLED. Michael Foods reached a settlement with the direct-purchaser plaintiff class; final court approval was issued November 20, 2017.
   - Amount: $75 million (Michael Foods' individual settlement). This brought the cumulative direct-purchaser settlement total (across all settling defendants to that point) to over $135–136 million.
   - Scope limits: the settlement covered direct-purchaser class claims only. It explicitly did NOT resolve claims by indirect purchasers of eggs, nor direct-purchaser claims that were on appeal at the time.
   - Timing note: this litigation and the underlying conduct alleged predate Post Holdings' acquisition of Michael Foods (Post acquired Michael Foods June 2, 2014); the settlement was reached and approved after Post's ownership began, but the alleged conduct occurred earlier.
   - Sources: https://www.postholdings.com/newsroom/post-holdings-settles-egg-antitrust-class-action-claims/ ; https://www.feedstuffs.com/livestock-and-poultry-market-news/post-holdings-settles-egg-antitrust-class-action-claims ; https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/lawsuit-news/michael-foods-pay-75m-settle-egg-price-fixing-class-action/ ; case docket reference via https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USCOURTS-paed-2_08-md-02002/USCOURTS-paed-2_08-md-02002-70

2. **HSUS FTC complaint re: Davidson's Safest Choice packaging**
   - Filed: October 14, 2016, by the Humane Society of the United States (HSUS) with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC).
   - Target: National Pasteurized Eggs, Inc. (maker of Davidson's Safest Choice; National Pasteurized Eggs was in the process of being acquired by Post Holdings/Michael Foods at the time the complaint was filed — the acquisition was announced September 1, 2016).
   - Allegation (as alleged by HSUS, not adjudicated): Davidson's carton packaging and website depicted "lush open pastures, a red barn and free-roaming hens," which HSUS alleged misrepresents production methods because — per the complaint's allegation — the pictured hens were, in reality, confined in battery cages "unable to spread their wings, venture outside, breathe 'fresh' air, or see natural sunlight." HSUS additionally alleged the company misleadingly claimed its pasteurization process "eliminates the risk of Salmonella."
   - Status: This was a complaint filed WITH the FTC (an administrative complaint asking the FTC to investigate), not a lawsuit — no search result located confirming any subsequent FTC investigation finding, consent order, or enforcement action, nor any court case arising from it. Status should be treated as: allegation filed, outcome/resolution NOT FOUND in this research pass.
   - Sources: https://www.foodsafetynews.com/2016/10/humane-society-says-egg-carton-artwork-is-false-advertising/ ; https://www.thepoultrysite.com/news/2016/10/egg-company-accused-of-misleading-consumers ; https://natlawreview.com/article/federal-trade-commission-complaint-targets-egg-supplier-ads ; complaint document at https://truthinadvertising.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/HSUS-Davidson-complaint.pdf

3. **FSIS recall — Michael Foods Inc. (M.G. Waldbaum dba Michael Foods Inc., Gaylord, MN establishment)**
   - Date: recall announced approximately July 1, 2024 (production date of affected lots: June 11, 2024).
   - Product: "FAIR MEADOW Foundations WHOLE EGGS with CITRIC ACID," 32-oz paperboard carton, use-by date "16 SEP 24," lot code 4162G.
   - Reason: misbranding / undeclared allergen — the liquid-egg "Breakfast Blend Scrambled Egg" production run inadvertently used unaccounted-for Whole Egg with Citric Acid cartons; the Breakfast Blend formulation contains milk (a dairy allergen) not declared on the Whole Egg with Citric Acid label.
   - Amount: approximately 4,620 pounds.
   - Distribution: shipped to restaurant/institutional consignees in Alabama, Idaho, Louisiana, Minnesota, Nebraska, New Mexico, Ohio, South Carolina, and Utah.
   - Illnesses: no confirmed adverse-reaction reports as of the recall notice.
   - Source: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/michael-foods-inc--recalls-fair-meadow-foundations-liquid-egg-products-due (direct fetch blocked with HTTP 403; details corroborated via https://spectrumnews1.com/oh/columbus/news/2024/07/02/liquid-egg-product-recall-u-s- and https://foodpoisoningbulletin.com/2024/fair-meadow-foundations-whole-liquid-eggs-recalled-for-milk/)

4. **FSIS recall — Cargill Kitchen Solutions (co-packer of Egg Beaters and Bob Evans liquid-egg products)**
   - Date: recall announced approximately April 3–4, 2025 (affected product produced March 12–13, 2025).
   - Establishment: Cargill Kitchen Solutions, Lake Odessa, Michigan.
   - Products affected: included Egg Beaters-branded liquid egg products AND Bob Evans-branded "Better'n Eggs Made with Real Egg Whites" (32-oz carton, USE BY AUG 10 2025) — i.e., this single recall event covered products under both the Egg Beaters and Bob Evans brand names, both owned by Post Holdings, both apparently produced at this Cargill-operated facility.
   - Reason: possible contamination with a cleaning solution containing sodium hypochlorite (an unapproved substance in the product).
   - Amount: approximately 212,268 pounds.
   - Recall class: Class III (FSIS assessment: use of the product should not cause adverse health consequences, or the risk is negligible).
   - Distribution: shipped to distributor locations in Ohio and Texas, and for foodservice use in Arizona, California, Colorado, Florida, Illinois, and Iowa; some reporting noted possible nationwide distribution.
   - Illnesses: no confirmed adverse-reaction reports.
   - Relationship note: this recall establishes that Cargill Kitchen Solutions (a Cargill subsidiary) manufactures/co-packs liquid egg products under both the Egg Beaters and Bob Evans brand names for Post Holdings — i.e., at least some Post-owned egg-brand production is contracted out to a third-party processor rather than run entirely through Michael Foods' own plants.
   - Sources: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/cargill-kitchen-solutions-recalls-liquid-egg-products-due-unapproved-substance (direct fetch blocked, HTTP 403) ; corroborated via https://www.today.com/food/recall/liquid-egg-recall-rcna198830 and https://www.npr.org/2025/04/04/nx-s1-5352329/liquid-eggs-recall-egg-beaters-bob-evans (fetch timed out; used search-result summary) and https://www.wattagnet.com/broilers-turkeys/food-safety/news/15741649/cargill-kitchen-solutions-recalls-liquid-egg-products (fetch blocked 403, used search snippet)

5. **Avian influenza — disclosed disease event (not enforcement, but a material operational/financial disclosure)**
   - December 9, 2024 press release: a Michael Foods third-party contracted egg-laying flock in Iowa tested positive for avian influenza.
   - Scale: approximately 4.5 million egg-laying hens at the affected facility, described as approximately 12% of Post's controlled egg supply (inclusive of owned and third-party contracted farms).
   - Company statement: Post affirmed its FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA outlook, stating the financial impact was believed to be within the guidance range's tolerances.
   - Source: https://www.postholdings.com/post-holdings-reports-avian-influenza-at-third-party-contracted-facility-and-affirms-fiscal-year-2025-adjusted-ebitda-outlook/

## Sourcing model
- Aggregator / mixed owned-and-contract-farm model. Post's own December 2024 disclosure describes Michael Foods' "controlled supply" as "inclusive of owned and third-party contracted farms," with the Iowa facility alone (4.5M hens) representing ~12% of that controlled supply — implying Post/Michael Foods' total controlled hen count is roughly in the 35–40 million range (not independently stated by Post; this is an inference from the 12% figure, not a company-disclosed total, and should be treated as a rough estimate, not a confirmed count).
- Contract manufacturing/co-packing: confirmed (see recall #4 above) that at least some Egg Beaters and Bob Evans liquid-egg-product manufacturing is done by Cargill Kitchen Solutions, a third-party (Cargill-owned) processor, not by Post/Michael Foods' own plants.
- Michael Foods also operates its own processing plant in Wakefield, Nebraska (Michael Foods Egg Products Company) per company/local economic-development sourcing. — Source: https://www.wakefieldne.com/economic-development/page/michael-foods-inc ; https://www.michaelfoods.com/about-us/history/
- Brand acquisition timeline relevant to sourcing/ownership chain:
  - Post Holdings acquired Michael Foods: completed June 2, 2014.
  - Post/Michael Foods acquired National Pasteurized Eggs, Inc. (maker of Davidson's Safest Choice): announced September 1, 2016.
  - Post Holdings acquired the Egg Beaters liquid-egg business from Conagra Brands: for $50.6 million; deal announced May 27, 2021, completed July 14, 2021. Multiple secondary sources describe Egg Beaters as subsequently run under Post's Bob Evans Farms division.
  - Post Holdings acquired Bob Evans Farms, Inc.: announced September 19, 2017 ($77/share, ~$1.5 billion); completed January 12, 2018.
  - Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001530950/000153095014000179/ex99-1michaelfoodsclosingpr.htm ; https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2016/09/01/868844/0/en/Post-Holdings-to-Acquire-National-Pasteurized-Eggs.html ; https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/POST-HOLDINGS-INC-9971428/news/Post-Holdings-Inc-acquired-Egg-Beaters-Liquid-Egg-Business-of-Conagra-Brands-Inc-for-50-6-milli-35856330/ ; https://www.cnbc.com/2017/09/19/post-holdings-to-buy-bob-evans-farms-for-1-point-5-billion.html ; https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/POST-HOLDINGS-INC-9971428/news/Post-Holdings-Inc-completed-the-acquisition-of-Bob-Evans-Farms-Inc--34854350/
