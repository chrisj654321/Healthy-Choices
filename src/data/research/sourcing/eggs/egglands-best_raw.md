## Housing systems

Per Eggland's Best company website (egglandsbest.com/faqs/animal-welfare, egglandsbest.com/products/cage-free-eggs, egglandsbest.com/product/organic-eggs — company-disclosed marketing copy, not independently verified):
- **Cage-Free**: company describes hens as having "ready access to sunlight, shelter, water, and nutritious proprietary feed."
- **Organic** (USDA-recognized term when certified): company states hens are "not kept in cages and are free to roam," with "sunlight, shade, shelter, an exercise area, fresh air."
- **Free Range** (USDA-recognized term): listed as a product line on egglandsbest.com product listing, alongside Large, Extra-Large, Jumbo, Hard-Cooked, Liquid Egg Whites.
- **Pasture Raised**: listed as a product line on egglandsbest.com. NOTE per brief: "pasture-raised" has no USDA legal definition. No certification (Certified Humane pasture-raised, AGW, etc.) was found in this research confirming a specific pasture-raised standard for this line — recorded here as a company marketing/product-line claim, not a verified housing standard.
- Company sells **conventional (non-cage-free, non-organic)** shell eggs as its base "Eggland's Best" product — the brand's flagship claim is nutritional (lower saturated fat, more vitamins), not housing-based, per FTC case history below.

Basis: company website product pages and FAQ (company disclosure), not third-party audited.

## Sourcing model

**Licensing/franchise model** — confirmed by multiple sources:
- Eggland's Best does not own hens; it licenses its brand and feed/production specifications to independent egg producers who become "franchisees." (Basis: company disclosure via egglandsbest.com/about-us, corroborated by trade press.)
- Named franchisee/producers identified in secondary sources: Cal-Maine Foods (described as the largest franchisee, and one of 13 Eggland's Best shareholders, holding exclusive license agreements for markets including New York City and parts of the Southeast/Southwest), Herbruck's Poultry Ranch, Braswell Family Farms, Kreher Family Egg Farms, Southwest Specialty Eggs. Source: trade press (feedstuffs.com, wattagnet.com, prnewswire.com), not Eggland's Best's own disclosure of its full franchisee list.
- Quality control per company: franchised producers submit monthly feed samples to an Eggland's Best lab for evaluation.
- Cornucopia Institute's scorecard entry independently characterizes this as "Corporate veiling, lack of transparency in business model" (Cornucopia's own descriptive language, recorded as their assessment, not verified independently here).

Basis: mix of company disclosure (franchise model, feed QC) and trade press identification of specific franchisee companies (Cal-Maine SEC filing exhibit, wattagnet.com, feedstuffs.com).
