## Corporate cage-free commitment (pledge / target / progress)

- **Original pledge**: Kroger announced July 12, 2016 (some secondary sources say March 2016 for initial signaling) a goal "to transition to a 100 percent cage-free egg supply chain by 2025." Exact quoted wording per secondary reporting: "As our customer base has been moving to cage-free at an increasing rate, Kroger's goal is to transition to a 100 percent cage-free egg supply chain by 2025." At the time of the pledge, secondary reporting states only 15% of Kroger's egg sales were cage-free. Source (secondary, quoting company statement): https://www.ciwf.com/media-and-news/blog/2016/03/kroger-goes-cage-free and https://614now.com/2016/food-drink/kroger-to-sell-only-cage-free-eggs-within-10-years — primary company PDF located but not text-extractable: https://www.thekrogerco.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/Statement-Cage-Free-Eggs.pdf
- **2022 revision (company disclosure, basis: company ESG report)**: Per secondary reporting on Kroger's ESG report, in 2022 Kroger stated it does not expect to achieve the 2025 100% cage-free commitment. Revised goal: transition to approximately 70% of eggs sold (by unit) to cage-free or higher-welfare standards by 2030, with interim milestones reported as 33% (2022), 41% (2024), 54% (2025), 61% (2028). Source: secondary reporting citing Kroger's ESG report, via WATTPoultry: https://www.wattagnet.com/egg/cage-free-laying-systems/article/15536685/walmart-kroger-will-not-meet-2025-cage-free-commitments
- **2022 milestone result reported**: Kroger's 2022 milestone target was 33%; secondary reporting states actual achieved was 33.6%, described as "exceeded." Source: secondary reporting (search-aggregated, exact article not individually confirmed by direct fetch) referencing Kroger's ESG action plan; primary performance table located at https://www.thekrogerco.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Kroger-Co-2023-ESG-Report_Final_Performance-Table-1.pdf (PDF; not text-extracted in this session — flagged as a gap).
- **2024 progress figures (company disclosure, basis: company ESG report)**: Secondary reporting states Kroger's 2024 ESG report showed 56% of total egg revenue and 43% of egg units from cage-free sources, described as exceeding Kroger's own revised 2024 goal. A separate secondary source states cage-free egg revenue "rebounded to almost 52%" by 2023. These two percentages (56% revenue-2024 vs ~52% revenue-2023) come from different secondary aggregations and were not cross-verified against the primary PDF in this session. Source: search-aggregated secondary reporting; primary source (unextracted PDF) as above.
- **Investment disclosure**: Secondary reporting states Kroger outlined a plan to invest $45 million in additional pricing, promotions, and agreements to support the cage-free/higher-welfare transition. Source: secondary reporting (Progressive Grocer / Grocery Dive aggregation), not independently confirmed against primary PDF.
- **Target met/extended/dropped**: Per all sources reviewed, the original 2025 100% target was NOT met and has been extended/revised (not dropped outright — replaced with the 70%-by-2030 goal described above). As of this research date (2026-07-28), the original 2025 deadline has passed.
- **Michigan state law context**: Michigan state law is reported to prohibit sale of eggs from caged hens beginning in 2025 (state regulatory fact, separate from Kroger's own voluntary pledge). Source: https://www.michigan.gov/ag/news/press-releases/2024/07/03/ag-nessel-urges-retailers-to-implement-clear-signage-on-egg-sourcing and https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/trump-administration-challenges-michigans-cage-154432473.html (Trump administration challenge to Michigan's law — noted as a lead/context fact, not further verified in this session).

## Supplier identity

- **Midwest Poultry Services** — publicly identified (via the July 2026 FDA-linked recall reporting above) as a supplier of eggs distributed under the Kroger and Simple Truth brand names (among others: Brookshire's, Country Morning, Sunups), from Texas farms. Source: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/egg-recall-salmonella-midwest-poultry-kroger/
- **Kipster / MPS Egg Farms** — Kipster (Dutch company) partnered with Kroger in 2022 (5-year licensing/franchise agreement per secondary reporting) to supply "carbon-neutral," cage-free eggs sold under the Simple Truth line, produced via a U.S. Kipster farm built in partnership with MPS Egg Farms. Secondary reporting (dated after the original partnership) states the Kipster/Kroger co-branding arrangement has since ended and Kipster is transitioning to sell eggs under its own brand name in Kroger-family stores (King Soopers, City Market, Pick 'n Save, Dillons, Fry's, Smith's, Mariano's, Metro Market) rather than co-branded with Simple Truth. Sources: https://www.foodnavigator-usa.com/Article/2024/08/28/Kipster-s-carbon-neutral-eggs-at-Kroger-paves-the-way-for-possible-US-franchise-expansion/ ; https://kipster.farm/faq/ ; https://www.feedstuffs.com/agribusiness-news/kipster-celebrates-world-s-first-carbon-neutral-eggs-in-the-u-s-
