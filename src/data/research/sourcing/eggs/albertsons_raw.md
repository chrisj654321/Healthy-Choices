## Housing systems

Albertsons/Safeway sells eggs under the Lucerne name across multiple housing systems — this is a retailer private label spanning several tiers, not a single housing claim:
- **Conventional (caged)**: Lucerne Grade AA Large Eggs (catalog SKU in scope) — no cage-free/organic claim in the product name.
- **Cage-free**: "Lucerne Farms Eggs Cage Free Large" (multiple pack sizes: 6, 12, 18 count) sold on albertsons.com. Sources: https://www.albertsons.com/shop/product-details.960309877.html ; https://www.albertsons.com/shop/product-details.960304348.html ; https://www.albertsons.com/shop/product-details.960273951.html
- Other Albertsons-family store brands also sell cage-free/free-range/organic lines under separate names: O Organics (organic, cage-free), Open Nature (cage-free and free-range variants). Sources: https://www.albertsons.com/shop/product-details.960079385.html ; https://www.albertsons.com/shop/product-details.960453266.html ; https://www.albertsons.com/shop/product-details.138350308.html
- "Cage-free" and "free-range" are USDA-recognized shell-egg terms; "pasture-raised" has no USDA legal definition. Note: the 2024 ASPCA Supermarket Scorecard (see below) describes an Albertsons-family "O Organics" egg option as "pasture raised" — recorded as reported by that source, not independently verified against USDA labeling rules for that specific SKU.

## Cage-free commitment (if retailer)

- **Original pledge**: Announced March 1, 2016. Albertsons Companies committed to working with suppliers toward sourcing 100% cage-free eggs, "based on available supply," for its retail shell-egg products by **2025**, across its banners (Safeway, Vons, Pavilions, Jewel-Osco, Shaw's, Acme, Tom Thumb, Randalls, United Supermarkets, Star Market, Carrs, and others). Company disclosure (primary press release). Sources: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/albertsons-companies-sets-goal-for-cage-free-eggs-by-2025-300228385.html ; https://www.meatpoultry.com/articles/13866-albertsons-companies-sets-goal-for-cage-free-eggs-by-2025 ; https://www.supermarketnews.com/dairy/albertsons-pledges-all-cage-free-eggs-by-2025
- **2017 expansion**: In March 2017, Albertsons Companies added retail **liquid eggs** (sold under the Lucerne and Open Nature brands, per PR Newswire) to its cage-free commitment, describing itself as "the only major supermarket to explicitly include liquid eggs in its cage-free commitment," covering "2,300+ stores that operate under 19 banners." Company disclosure (primary press release). Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/albertsons-companies-adds-liquid-eggs-to-cage-free-commitment-300430005.html ; https://www.supermarketnews.com/independents-regional-grocers/albertsons-companies-adds-liquid-eggs-to-cage-free-commitment
- **2023 progress figure (company disclosure, basis: company reporting)**: Per search-indexed content from Albertsons' own "Animal Well-Being" impact page (direct fetch of the live page returned HTTP 404/500 in this session — recorded as reported via search-engine snippet of that URL, not independently re-verified by direct page load), "more than 60% of liquid and shell eggs available in [Albertsons] stores were sourced from cage-free environments" as of 2023. The same source states Albertsons' O Organics and Open Nature brands "exclusively offer cage-free shell and liquid eggs" and that all Own Brands hardboiled eggs are cage-free-sourced. Source (page returned error on direct fetch): https://www.albertsonscompanies.com/our-impact/products/animal-well-being/default.aspx
- **Third-party tracker (ASPCA Supermarket Scorecard, 2024, published August 2024)**: Albertsons received an overall grade of **C** (320 out of 900 total points). On the cage-free-egg-specific components: Policy score 100/100 ("complete" cage-free policy — met all required policy components), Progress-reporting score 48 (described by the scorecard as indicating "less than half of published progress relates to a complete policy"), Store-brand score 63, combined "Laying Hens" category score 211 out of 300. The scorecard notes Albertsons (along with Kroger and Safeway) showed "progress on transitioning to cage-free eggs" that contributed to an overall score increase versus prior scorecards, and lists Albertsons among nine retailers offering "at least one fully cage-free store brand" of eggs. Source: https://www.aspca.org/supermarketscorecard
- **Target met/extended/dropped — status as of this research date (2026-07-28), original 2025 deadline has now passed**: No primary Albertsons source located in this session explicitly stating the 2025 100%-cage-free target was met, formally extended, or formally withdrawn. One secondary/advocacy source — a Substack post ("Crunch Time for Cage-Free," farmanimalwelfare.substack.com) — states in its own editorial voice that Albertsons "quietly removed their pledges to reach 100% cage-free" while continuing to share progress publicly, naming Albertsons alongside Walmart, Kroger, Target, Subway, Best Western, and Marriott as companies that have "backtracked." This is a secondary, single-author advocacy blog post, not a primary company disclosure or an established tracker methodology — **flagged as a Lead, not confirmed as fact.** The same post states, without a specific denominator source cited in the excerpt reviewed, that "over 1,400 of the 2,700+ companies with pledges have already implemented them successfully" (an industry-wide figure, not Albertsons-specific). Source: https://farmanimalwelfare.substack.com/p/crunch-time-for-cage-free
- No Albertsons press release or ESG report was located in this session with a company-disclosed percentage more recent than the 2023 ~60% figure above (i.e., no 2024, 2025, or 2026 company-published cage-free percentage was found and confirmed).

## Sourcing model / supplier identity

- Lucerne is a retailer private-label (store) brand of Albertsons Companies (Safeway heritage brand); Albertsons does not itself own laying flocks — it sources shell and liquid eggs from third-party suppliers/producers who pack under the Lucerne name. No specific named supplier(s) for the conventional Lucerne Grade AA line were disclosed in sources reviewed (Albertsons' public statements name the cage-free supplier-certification requirement — see Certified Humane hit above — but do not name individual egg suppliers/producers).
- **Corporate context (per assignment instructions, relevance check only)**: Albertsons' proposed $24.6 billion merger with The Kroger Co. was blocked by a federal judge (U.S. District Court, District of Oregon) on December 10, 2024, on FTC antitrust grounds; a Washington State (King County) court separately ruled the merger unlawful around the same time; Kroger subsequently terminated the merger agreement. No source reviewed connects the blocked merger to Albertsons' egg-sourcing or cage-free commitments — recorded here only per instructions, not chased further. Sources: https://www.cnn.com/2024/12/10/business/kroger-albertsons-merger-ruling ; https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/12/statement-ftc-victory-securing-halt-kroger-albertsons-grocery-merger ; https://www.atg.wa.gov/news/news-releases/judge-blocks-kroger-albertsons-merger-following-ag-ferguson-challenge
