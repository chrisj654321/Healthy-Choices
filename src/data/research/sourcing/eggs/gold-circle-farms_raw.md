## Sourcing model / supplier identity

- Hidden Villa Ranch is corporately operated by **Luberski, Inc.** (confirmed via the San Diego City Attorney settlement, which names "Luberski, Inc." doing business as Hidden Villa Ranch, and via the Law360 TTAB case caption "Luberski Inc dba Hidden Villa Ranch"). Corporate offices in Fullerton, CA. Sources: https://www.nbcsandiego.com/news/local/company-to-shell-out-201k-eggs-for-consumer-protection-settlement/1994965/ ; https://www.law360.com/ttab_cases/58d6c591dda6082cf500004e
- Per a WATTPoultry/WATTAgNet company profile (found via search, direct fetch returned HTTP 403 in this session — recorded as reported by the search-indexed snippet, not independently re-verified by direct page load): "Hidden Villa Ranch is the name behind brand names such as California Sunshine, California Ranch Fresh, Perfectly Fresh, Horizon Organic and Gold Circle Farms," with corporate offices in Fullerton, CA, and business described as marketing, trading, wholesaling, producing, exporting, and distribution of shell, liquid, and frozen eggs plus cheese and produce. This is the only source located that explicitly places "Gold Circle Farms" under the Hidden Villa Ranch brand portfolio. Source: https://www.wattagnet.com/top-poultry-companies/company/hidden-villa-ranch (fetch attempt returned 403; content above from search-engine snippet of the same URL)
- **Nature's Yoke relationship — checked, not found.** Nature's Yoke is a separate, Weaver-family-operated egg business headquartered in New Holland, PA (est. 1963, incorporated as "Nature's Yoke" in 2000; George Weaver III listed as President/CEO). No source located connecting Nature's Yoke to Hidden Villa Ranch, Luberski Inc., or Gold Circle Farms — the two appear to be unrelated, geographically distinct companies (Fullerton, CA vs. New Holland, PA). **This supports treating the catalog's mapping of a Gold Circle Farms product to Nature's Yoke as a likely data error** — Gold Circle Farms is sourced (per the WATTPoultry description above) to Hidden Villa Ranch/Luberski Inc., not to Nature's Yoke. Sources: https://naturesyoke.com/the-natures-yoke-legacy/ ; https://naturesyoke.com/about/
