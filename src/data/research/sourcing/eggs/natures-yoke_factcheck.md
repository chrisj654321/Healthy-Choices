## Corporate ownership findings (incl. any error in OUR catalog)

**This directly bears on the catalog data-bug flagged in the Gold Circle Farms file.** Nature's Yoke is a Weaver-family-operated business (Westfield Egg Farm, Inc., New Holland, PA) with a documented, self-described brand portfolio (Nature's Yoke, Utopihen Farms, Rosie's Farm Fresh, Elevated Delights, Power Scrub) that I confirmed myself via westfieldeggfarm.com/brands — **Gold Circle Farms does not appear on this list, and no source anywhere (company site, trade press, business directories) connects the two companies.** Gold Circle Farms is consistently sourced elsewhere to Luberski, Inc./Hidden Villa Ranch (Fullerton, CA) — a completely separate, geographically distinct corporate entity.

**Verdict: the catalog's mapping of a Gold Circle Farms product to `natures-yoke` is very likely a data error.** It should be corrected to attribute Gold Circle Farms to Hidden Villa Ranch/Luberski Inc. instead. See the Gold Circle Farms fact-check file for the full corroborating evidence chain (I was unable to load a single primary corporate/trademark filing tying Gold Circle Farms to Luberski, so this stays at "very likely wrong, strongly corroborated by convergent secondary sources" rather than "confirmed via primary record" — but the convergence, combined with zero counter-evidence, is strong enough that this should be treated as an actionable correction, not left as an open question).

## Scorecard scope (which entity/line was actually rated)

Cornucopia's "Nature's Yoke (Westfield Egg Farm)" score (1115/1700, 4-star) explicitly scopes to the **organic line** of a mixed producer that also sells conventional eggs on separate properties — I confirmed this scope language myself on the live page. This is NOT a whole-company score; it is specific to the organic product line, consistent with how Cornucopia's directory generally works (organic-only scorecard).
