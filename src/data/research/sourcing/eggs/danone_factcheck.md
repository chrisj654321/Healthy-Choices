## Corporate ownership findings (incl. any error in OUR catalog)

**This is the priority finding for this company.** As of 2026, Horizon Organic is **majority-owned by Platinum Equity** (private-equity firm), with **Danone S.A. holding a minority stake** — confirmed via Platinum Equity's own April 2, 2024 completion press release (primary) plus Food Dive trade-press reporting on the January 2024 announcement (secondary, corroborating). No subsequent ownership change was found searching as of 2026-07-29.

**Any catalog/company record that attributes Horizon Organic simply to "Danone" as full/sole owner in 2026 is factually wrong and should be corrected** to reflect Platinum Equity as majority owner. This has downstream consequences: any political-spending, lobbying, or corporate-influence figures currently attached to "Danone" for Horizon-related purposes need re-routing to Platinum Equity as the controlling entity, with Danone retained only as a minority-stake footnote.

**Unresolved and consequential**: whether the shell-egg licensing business specifically transferred to Platinum Equity, remains with Danone, or sits in a separate arrangement with Hidden Villa Ranch/Braswell Foods was **not** resolved by either agent — Platinum Equity's own release is silent on eggs entirely. Any egg-specific ownership claim beyond "Danone doesn't fully own this anymore" should be hedged as unconfirmed.
