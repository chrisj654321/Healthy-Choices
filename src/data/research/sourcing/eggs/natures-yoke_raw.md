## Housing systems

- Nature's Yoke sells three named free-range lines per its own site and Westfield's brand page (both fetched directly): **Organic**, **Legacy**, and **Omega-3** — all described as "Free-Range" and "100% Certified Humane."
- Sister Westfield brand **Utopihen Farms** is separately marketed as "all pasture-raised" (per westfieldeggfarm.com/brands, fetched directly) — flagged per brief that "pasture-raised" has no USDA legal definition; this is a Westfield-family-brand marketing claim, distinct from Nature's Yoke's own "Free-Range" (a USDA-recognized shell-egg term) framing. Utopihen is a different brand/SKU than the catalog product researched here (Nature's Yoke), noted for context on the parent company's brand portfolio only.
- Cornucopia's scorecard (organic line, 1115/1700, 4-star) provides the most granular, independently-scored housing detail available: fixed (non-mobile) housing structures of 10,000–21,000 birds each, with "exits on 2-3 sides"; 2 sq ft/bird indoor and 2 sq ft/hen outdoor space; non-rotated outdoor land maintained at "50-70% grass, 30-50% trees/shrubs, 15-20% bare ground"; birds introduced to outdoor access at 22 weeks old; both indoor and outdoor enrichments including hay bales and planted crops; beak trimming performed at the hatchery (not on-farm); forced molting not allowed.
- Business/sourcing-model framing (per naturesyoke.com and westfieldeggfarm.com, both fetched directly): Nature's Yoke states it "does not own the land or the hens" — the farmers "own the birds, buy their own feed, own the farms, and run their own businesses," with Nature's Yoke/Westfield operating as a contract packer/marketer partnering with independently-owned family farms (see Sourcing model below for farm-count detail).

## Sourcing model

**Contract-farm network of small, family-owned farms — not a single-farm or corporate-industrial operation:**
- Corporate entity: Westfield Egg Farm, Inc., headquartered at 165 N. Shirk Rd., New Holland, PA 17557. Described consistently (own site, PFMA member directory, secondary press) as a "fourth-generation, family-owned specialty egg packaging company," founded in 1963 by George Weaver (per naturesyoke.com, fetched directly) — one secondary source (klimsonls.com) gives "1963," another search-indexed snippet gives "1962"; not reconciled to a single exact founding year in this pass.
- Current leadership per naturesyoke.com (fetched directly): George Weaver III (third generation) is President and CEO; George Weaver IV (fourth generation) is involved in marketing/branding.
- The "Nature's Yoke" brand itself launched in 2000, per naturesyoke.com (fetched directly); the underlying company/farm-network relationship predates the brand name.
- Farm network: per a secondary source (FreshDirect blog, "Visiting the Pastured Hens of Lancaster, PA") and corroborating search-indexed content, Nature's Yoke's eggs come from "25 small, family-owned farms, all within a 2 1/2-hour range of New Holland" (PA). This specific "25 farms" figure was not independently re-confirmed against a Nature's Yoke or Westfield primary-source page in this pass — flagged as needing direct confirmation.
- Westfield's own "Farm Relations Team" is described (per westfieldeggfarm.com, fetched directly, "About" page content) as building relationships with new/existing family farms "through regular communication, inspections, and proper documentation, coordinating flock production to meet customer and market needs."
- Westfield Egg Farm owns four consumer brands total (per westfieldeggfarm.com/brands, fetched directly): **Nature's Yoke** (free-range: Organic/Legacy/Omega-3), **Utopihen Farms** (marketed "all pasture-raised": Organic/Original/Soy-Free/Duck), **Rosie's Farm Fresh** (free-range, budget-positioned), and **Elevated Delights** (quail eggs, described as Westfield's "newest brand"). A fifth listing, "Power Scrub," is egg-washing equipment sold to other small/mid-scale poultry farmers (500–5,000 laying hens) — not a consumer egg brand.
- **On the Amish/Mennonite sourcing question specifically flagged in the brief:** no Nature's Yoke or Westfield primary source fetched in this pass explicitly states its contract farms are Amish- or Mennonite-operated. Lancaster County, PA (the stated sourcing region) is widely known as a center of Amish/Mennonite agriculture, and a search for "Weavertown Amish Mennonite Church" surfaced in results tied to the Weaver family name/region, but this is circumstantial (a place/family-name association) and not a sourced claim that any specific contract farm is Amish- or Mennonite-run. **Recorded as an open gap, not a fact** — do not assert Amish/Mennonite sourcing without a direct source.
