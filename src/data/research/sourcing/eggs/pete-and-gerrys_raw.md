## Housing systems

Per company website (peteandgerrys.com) and press releases (company-disclosed marketing copy):
- **Organic** (USDA-recognized certification): base product line, USDA Certified Organic feed.
- **Free Range** (USDA-recognized term): listed as a product line ("Organic, Free-Range, and Pasture-Raised Eggs" per site tagline).
- **Pasture Raised**: company markets "Organic Pasture Raised Eggs" (launched per PR Newswire release) with a stated "108 square feet of outdoor space per hen," attributed by the company to meeting "Certified Humane Pasture-Raised standards" — i.e., this brand DOES attach a named third-party certification (Certified Humane) to its pasture-raised claim, per company/press statements. NOT independently verified against certifiedhumane.org's own standard document in this research pass.
- Nellie's Free Range Eggs (sister brand, same parent company) markets a free-range line; specific housing detail for Nellie's beyond the "free-range" labeling was not separately researched in depth in this pass (see Gaps).
- Company acquired Farmers Hen House (per Jan 2025 press release) — expanding the parent's brand portfolio; housing systems for Farmers Hen House brand not researched in this pass (outside the two brands scoped for this company).

Basis: company website, PR Newswire/BusinessWire press releases (company disclosure).

## Sourcing model

**Contract-farms / co-op-style network, per company and Cornucopia disclosure**: Cornucopia's scorecard describes the brand as partnering "with nearly 20 other family farms throughout the Northeast" (as of the March 10, 2023 page) and, in the 2024-dated separate listing, states "Multiple farms supply this brand, which is family owned and operated, with a close relationship" and "The majority of suppliers have flock sizes less than 20,000 birds." Basis: Cornucopia Institute's third-party scorecard assessment (which states it is based on producer questionnaires/investigation), not Pete and Gerry's own public disclosure of its exact farm count or contract terms in this pass.

Company acquired Farmers Hen House (announced January 2025, per BusinessWire) — indicates the parent company is consolidating additional egg brands/production under its umbrella; this expands the corporate structure beyond the two brands (Pete & Gerry's, Nellie's Free Range) scoped for this research.
