## CRITICAL OWNERSHIP FLAG — read before using this brand as "Danone"

**Danone is NOT the current full owner of Horizon Organic as of 2026.** Per a company press release (Platinum Equity, primary source, fetched directly) and trade-press reporting (Food Dive, fetched directly):
- Danone announced Jan. 3, 2024 it would sell Horizon Organic and Wallaby to private-equity firm **Platinum Equity**.
- The acquisition **completed April 2, 2024** (Platinum Equity's own press release, fetched directly), with Platinum Equity taking **majority ownership**.
- Food Dive's Jan. 3, 2024 reporting states explicitly: **"Danone will retain a minority stake in the business."**
- No subsequent sale of Platinum Equity's stake was found in this pass.
- **Current (2026) ownership, per available sources: Platinum Equity (majority owner) with Danone S.A. holding a minority stake.** Any characterization of Horizon Organic as simply "a Danone brand" would be a factual error as of 2026 — this needs to be reflected downstream (fact-checker/legal-writer) as "Platinum Equity-owned, Danone minority stake," not "Danone-owned."

Sources: https://www.platinumequity.com/news/platinum-equity-completes-acquisition-of-horizon-organic-and-wallaby-from-danone/ ; https://www.fooddive.com/news/danone-to-sell-horizon-organic-and-wallaby-brands-to-pe-firm/703487/

## Sourcing model

- **Brand-licensing / co-packer model, not a single-farm or direct-owned operation.** Horizon Organic's shell eggs are produced and distributed under license, not by Danone (or now Platinum Equity) directly:
  - **Hidden Villa Ranch** (legal entity: Luberski, Inc.; headquartered Fullerton, CA) is identified across multiple sources as "the exclusive licensee of Horizon Organic eggs," producing and distributing them nationally. Hidden Villa Ranch's own site (hiddenvilla.com, fetched directly) confirms this licensing relationship. Hidden Villa Ranch is also described (secondary source, WATTPoultry/incredibleegg.org listings) as the parent/producer behind other egg brands including "The Country Hen" and "NestFresh."
  - Separately, **Braswell Foods** is named by certifiedhumane.org (primary certifying-body source, fetched directly) as a Certified-Humane-certified producer of a "Horizon Organic Eggs" line, alongside its own house brands. This is a second named producer for a "Horizon Organic Eggs" product; not reconciled against the Hidden Villa Ranch licensing claim in this pass (see flag in Certification section).
  - No single-farm or direct-owned egg operation for Horizon was identified; the brand functions as a marketing/label layer over one or more third-party egg producers.
- Danone's own dairy-side sourcing model (per Platinum Equity's acquisition press release, fetched directly) is described as working "with more than 600 family farmers across the U.S." for its **dairy** (milk/yogurt/cheese/butter) business — this figure is dairy-specific and was not stated to include or exclude the egg supply chain; not treated as an egg-sourcing fact.
