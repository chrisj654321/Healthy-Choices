## Sourcing model / supplier identity

- **Ownership chain**: Michael Foods, Inc. (HQ Hopkins/Minnetonka, MN) is a subsidiary of Post Holdings, Inc. Post Holdings announced the acquisition of Michael Foods for $2.45 billion; the deal was announced in 2014. Sources: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/post-holdings-acquires-michael-foods-for-245-billion (referenced via search); https://www.vendingmarketwatch.com/management/news/11409677/post-holdings-acquires-michael-foods-for-245-billion ; https://www.startribune.com/post-to-buy-michael-foods-for-2-45b/255641511
- **Current ownership (2026)**: Michael Foods remains a Post Holdings subsidiary per Post Holdings' own 2025 Annual Report and current news releases (e.g., Post Holdings-branded Michael Foods facility-expansion announcements dated 2025-2026). Source: https://www.postholdings.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Post-Holdings-2025-Annual-Report-FINAL.pdf ; https://www.michaelfoods.com/about-us/news/post-holdings-michael-foods-business-announces-expansion-of-norwalk-iowa-manufacturing-facility/
- **Packer vs. flock-owner distinction**: Per Post Holdings' 2025 Annual Report (as summarized in search results), Post Holdings' egg-products operations include thirteen egg-products production facilities in the U.S., "some of which are fully integrated from the maintenance of laying flocks through the processing of egg products" — implying not all facilities are integrated flock-to-processing; some function as processing/packing operations receiving eggs from outside flocks. This directly bears on the "Crystal Farms Refrigerated Distribution Co." naming — the corporate name itself signals a distribution/packing function rather than a flock-owning identity. The precise flock-ownership status of the specific eggs packed under the Crystal Farms label was not confirmed in sources reviewed. Source: https://www.postholdings.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Post-Holdings-2025-Annual-Report-FINAL.pdf (referenced via search summary; not independently re-fetched in full in this session)
- Crystal Farms is listed alongside Better'n Eggs, All Whites, and Simply Potatoes as retail brands within Michael Foods' portfolio. Source: search-aggregated (grocery.com company profile), not independently re-verified by direct fetch: https://www.grocery.com/michael-foods-inc/

HQ listed: Hopkins, MN. **This rates the "Abbotsford Egg Products" line, not "Crystal Farms."** Cornucopia states the only points earned (20) came from an American Humane certification claim covering some eggs; all other criteria (ownership transparency, organic commitment, flock size, housing, welfare practices) scored zero, with Cornucopia noting "no producers in this category were willing to participate in Cornucopia's research." Advocacy/editorial framing (not a measured score) is present on the same page describing industrial-scale, no-meaningful-outdoor-access operations generally — flagged here as characterization, not fact.
