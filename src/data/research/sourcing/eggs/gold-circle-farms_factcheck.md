## Corporate ownership findings (incl. any error in OUR catalog)

**Priority finding, confirmed by independent verification effort from both directions (this file and the Nature's Yoke file):**

- Every source that names a corporate parent for Gold Circle Farms points to **Luberski, Inc. (dba Hidden Villa Ranch)**, Fullerton, CA — never to Nature's Yoke/Westfield Egg Farm, New Holland, PA.
- Nature's Yoke's own self-described brand portfolio (confirmed by me directly on westfieldeggfarm.com/brands) lists five brands and Gold Circle Farms is not one of them.
- No source of any kind — company page, trade press, business directory, litigation record — connects Gold Circle Farms to Nature's Yoke, the Weaver family, or Westfield Egg Farm.
- The counter-evidence (Gold Circle Farms → Luberski/Hidden Villa Ranch) itself rests only on **secondary sources** — a WATTPoultry page that returned HTTP 403 on two independent fetch attempts (by two different agents), a 2009 news article, and convergent-but-unloadable business-directory snippets (Bloomberg, trademarks.justia.com, both 403'd for me). **No primary corporate filing, trademark registration, or Luberski/Hidden Villa Ranch own-site page naming "Gold Circle Farms" was successfully loaded by either agent.**

**Verdict: the catalog's mapping of a Gold Circle Farms product to `natures-yoke` is very likely wrong and should be corrected to attribute it to Hidden Villa Ranch/Luberski Inc.** This conclusion is well-supported by convergent, independent secondary sourcing and a total absence of any counter-evidence — but it falls short of the strictest "VERIFIED via primary source" bar because every specific primary candidate (WATTAgNet, Bloomberg, USPTO/trademarks.justia.com) was blocked on every attempt by both agents. Treat this as a high-confidence, action-worthy correction, not a fully closed VERIFIED fact — ideally someone with authenticated/paid access to WATTAgNet, Bloomberg terminal, or USPTO TESS should do one more confirming pass before the catalog is silently corrected, though the practical risk of leaving the current wrong mapping in place is clearly worse than the risk of this correction being wrong.
