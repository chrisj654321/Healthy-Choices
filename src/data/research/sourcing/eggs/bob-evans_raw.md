## Housing systems
- NOT DISCLOSED at the brand-packaging/product level in sources reviewed. Bob Evans egg-white products are processed/liquid products; no source located that discloses the housing system (cage-free, free-range, conventional) of the source-flock hens specifically for Bob Evans-branded egg products.
- Manufacturing/production relationship: at least one Bob Evans-branded liquid egg product ("Bob Evans Better'n Eggs Made with Real Egg Whites") is co-packed by Cargill Kitchen Solutions (Lake Odessa, Michigan) — confirmed via the April 2025 FSIS recall event (see Enforcement section below and full detail in post-holdings_raw.md). This establishes a production-facility fact but does NOT by itself establish hen housing conditions.
- Parent company Post Holdings' general animal-welfare statement (company-authored, not third-party verified) specifically names Bob Evans Farms as a participant in "Protein PACT," described as an industry collective-action initiative on animal protein sustainability broadly — this is a stated industry-initiative membership, not a housing-system certification or disclosure. — Source: https://www.postholdings.com/home/sustainability/sourcing/animal-welfare/

## Enforcement / litigation

1. **FSIS recall — Cargill Kitchen Solutions (co-packer of Bob Evans and Egg Beaters liquid-egg products)**
   - Date: recall announced approximately April 3–4, 2025 (affected product produced March 12–13, 2025).
   - Establishment: Cargill Kitchen Solutions, Lake Odessa, Michigan.
   - Bob Evans product affected: "Bob Evans Better'n Eggs Made with Real Egg Whites," 32-oz (2-lb) carton, USE BY AUG 10 2025. (The same recall also covered Egg Beaters-branded product — see post-holdings_raw.md for the combined recall detail.)
   - Reason: possible contamination with a cleaning solution containing sodium hypochlorite (an unapproved substance).
   - Amount (combined, both brands): approximately 212,268 pounds.
   - Recall class: Class III (FSIS assessment: use of the product should not cause adverse health consequences, or the risk is negligible).
   - Distribution: shipped to distributor locations in Ohio and Texas, and for foodservice use in Arizona, California, Colorado, Florida, Illinois, and Iowa; some reporting noted possible nationwide distribution.
   - Illnesses: no confirmed adverse-reaction reports.
   - Sources: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/cargill-kitchen-solutions-recalls-liquid-egg-products-due-unapproved-substance (direct fetch blocked, HTTP 403) ; corroborated via https://www.today.com/food/recall/liquid-egg-recall-rcna198830 and https://www.npr.org/2025/04/04/nx-s1-5352329/liquid-eggs-recall-egg-beaters-bob-evans (fetch timed out; used search-result summary)

2. **Egg-industry antitrust litigation (Michael Foods, In re Processed Egg Products Antitrust Litigation)**

No source located naming Bob Evans Farms itself as a party to this or any other egg-antitrust litigation. Full detail in post-holdings_raw.md.

## Sourcing model
- Bob Evans Farms' retail/packaged egg products (including egg whites) are produced through Post Holdings' refrigerated retail business, which combines Bob Evans with Post's pre-existing egg/potato/cheese brands (Crystal Farms, Simply Potatoes, etc.). Michael Foods' egg-processing capacity underlies much of this refrigerated retail egg supply per company/trade descriptions, though the specific liquid-egg-white product line has at least partial production contracted to Cargill Kitchen Solutions (see Enforcement #1 above) — meaning Bob Evans-branded egg products are sourced through a mixed owned (Michael Foods) / third-party co-packer (Cargill Kitchen Solutions) model, not a single-facility or single-farm model.
- Bob Evans Farms operates its own manufacturing sites in Ohio, Michigan, and Texas for ready-to-eat foods alongside Simply Potatoes, Egg Beaters, and Owens Sausage products, per trade-press description. — Source: https://www.wakefieldne.com/economic-development/page/michael-foods-inc (Michael Foods Wakefield, NE plant reference) ; https://preview.bnpmedia.com/rff-december-2024/feature-bob-evans/ (Bob Evans manufacturing-sites reference, via search snippet)
- Corporate ownership chain: Bob Evans Farms, Inc. (packaged foods business) acquired by Post Holdings — acquisition announced September 19, 2017 for $77/share (~$1.5 billion total), completed January 12, 2018. Note the acquired entity is distinct from the separately-sold Bob Evans restaurant chain (sold to Golden Gate Capital around the same period) — the two "Bob Evans" businesses split apart and were sold to different buyers; only the packaged-foods/retail business (the one making egg whites) is under Post Holdings. — Sources: https://www.cnbc.com/2017/09/19/post-holdings-to-buy-bob-evans-farms-for-1-point-5-billion.html ; https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/POST-HOLDINGS-INC-9971428/news/Post-Holdings-Inc-completed-the-acquisition-of-Bob-Evans-Farms-Inc--34854350/ ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/33769/000119312518009424/d441070d8k.htm
