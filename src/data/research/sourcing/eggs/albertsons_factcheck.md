## Cage-free pledge: primary-source status
This is the strongest result of the four retailer pledges checked: the original March 2016 pledge, with its exact "based on available supply" qualifier, is **VERIFIED directly against Albertsons' own PR Newswire press release**. The 2017 liquid-egg expansion and 2023 ~60% progress figure remain unverified (not re-fetched / page broken, respectively) but were not contradicted by anything found.

## Scorecard scope (which line was actually rated vs our catalog SKU)
Cornucopia rates "O Organics," NOT "Lucerne" — confirmed directly (score 30/1700, 1-star). No Cornucopia egg score exists for Lucerne at all. Certified Humane applies only to Lucerne Cage-Free/O-Organic/Open Nature, explicitly excluding the conventional Lucerne Grade AA catalog SKU — confirmed directly, though the confirming source is from 2012 (STALE).

## Corrections to the raw file (factual errors found)
- **Most important correction**: The raw file states "no source reviewed names Albertsons as a party (plaintiff or defendant)" to the processed-egg antitrust MDL. This appears to be incorrect — strong, convergent (though not primary-document-loaded) evidence shows Albertsons was one of 12 retailer Direct Action Plaintiffs in that exact case, alongside Kroger (whose plaintiff status the raw Kroger file already documents from the same litigation). This should be corrected from "not a party" to "reported as a co-plaintiff with Kroger and 10 other retailers; not primary-confirmed this session." This is a meaningful gap-fill, not a tone change — the original research missed a real party-status fact that should have been findable given it applies to a company already covered as a plaintiff in the sibling Kroger file.
- The Certified Humane scope confirmation (claim #4) should carry a STALE flag alongside VERIFIED — the only source is a 2012 article, and 13+ years is a long time for a certification scope to go unconfirmed by anything more recent.

## Claims that must NOT be used
- Do NOT state Albertsons has "abandoned" or "dropped" its cage-free pledge as an established fact — this rests entirely on one Substack post with no corroboration found, exactly as the raw file already (correctly) flags.
- Do NOT state Albertsons was NOT a party to the processed-egg antitrust MDL — the evidence found this session points the other way (co-plaintiff with Kroger), even though it isn't primary-confirmed.
- Do NOT apply the Certified Humane cage-free/organic-only scope finding to any Lucerne product year beyond ~2012 without flagging it as a stale, unrefreshed data point.
