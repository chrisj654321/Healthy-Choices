## Housing systems
- Company markets multiple product lines with different housing claims: "free-range" (USDA-recognized term, company's core/flagship line), "organic free-range" (USDA Certified Organic + free-range), "heritage," and "pastured"/"pasture-raised" per Cornucopia's page — note "pasture-raised" has no USDA legal definition, so any such claim is a company marketing claim, not a regulated term. — Source: https://www.cornucopia.org/scorecard/eggs/happy-egg/
- AHA (American Humane Certified) press materials describe the certified standard as 21.8 sq ft of outdoor space per hen during daytime, with sheltered access at night/inclement weather — this describes the AHA certification standard as reported in 2015 trade press, not independently confirmed against AHA's current standards document. — Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-happy-egg-co-becomes-first-commercial-free-range-egg-producer-in-the-united-states-to-achieve-humane-certification-from-american-humane-association-300026283.html

## Sourcing model
- Contract-farm aggregator model. Happy Egg USA contracts with family farms; on October 29, 2024 Happy Egg completed a merger with/acquisition of Egg Innovations (previously owned by private-equity firm Skyline Global Partners), which itself operates via contract farms across Indiana, Illinois, Michigan, Ohio, Kentucky, Iowa, Minnesota, and Wisconsin, marketed partly under the "Blue Sky Family Farms" brand. Combined post-merger flock size stated as approximately 3.7 million hens. The merger also brought Happy Egg a feed mill and a dedicated packing/distribution center (Warsaw, Indiana). — Source: https://www.agriculturedive.com/news/happy-egg-merges-with-regenerative-supplier-egg-innovations/731489/ ; https://talkbusiness.net/2024/10/happy-egg-in-rogers-merges-with-egg-innovations/ ; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/happy-egg-strengthens-position-as-leading-egg-brand-with-integration-of-egg-innovations-302290413.html
- Corporate history/ownership: The Happy Egg Company originated as a brand of Noble Foods (UK, owned by the Dean family) — Noble Foods is the largest egg company in the UK. The US arm separated from Noble Foods/UK ownership around March 2019 and now operates independently, headquartered in Rogers, Arkansas. Search results describe current ownership as a privately-held, family-owned US company (no private-equity or public-company ownership found for Happy Egg USA itself as of this research; the PE-owned entity in the 2024 deal, Skyline Global Partners, was the seller of Egg Innovations, not an owner of Happy Egg). — Source: https://legalclarity.org/who-owns-happy-eggs-noble-foods-and-the-dean-family/ ; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/noble-foods-owned-happy-egg-co-transitions-to-us-based-management-team-to-drive-next-phase-of-growth-300400615.html ; https://talkbusiness.net/2018/05/egg-supplier-moves-u-s-headquarters-from-san-francisco-to-rogers/
