  plaintiff (a common outcome in this style of labeling strike suit) rather

  VERIFIED-SECONDARY: DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE**, not "pending."

Both corrections matter for Stage 4: neither suit should be described as
"ongoing" or "pending" — both resolved (one settled, one dismissed with
prejudice) years ago, and the court for Jones needs to be fixed to E.D.N.Y.

D&W ended the supplier relationship afterward. The specific claim that

else in this item is now VERIFIED.
