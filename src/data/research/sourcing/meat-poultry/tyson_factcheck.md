## PRIORITY (shared with kraft-heinz_raw.md) — Broiler chicken antitrust roles

**Claim:** Tyson Breeders, Inc. is a named party in *In re Broiler Chicken
Antitrust Litigation* (N.D. Ill., 1:16-cv-08637); raw file already
identifies Tyson as a confirmed defendant/settling producer.

**UPHELD — VERIFIED at effectively primary-source strength.** Independent
WebSearch cross-checked Tyson's defendant status against three separate,
independent listings of the case's defendant/settling-producer roster
(Lockridge Grindal Nauen case tracker, and a 2025 court-order summary
naming defendants still in litigation: Case, Claxton, George's, Raeford,
Koch, Mar-Jac, Marshall Durbin, Perdue, Pilgrim's, Sanderson, Simmons,
**Tyson**, Wayne). Tyson appears on every version of the defendant list
found, with zero contradicting source. No doubt on Tyson's role — this was
never in question per the brief, and this fact-check confirms it stays that
way.

**For companion context (full resolution in kraft-heinz_factcheck.md):**
Kraft Heinz Foods Company, also a named party in the same docket, is
**NOT** on any defendant list found — it is the purchaser/plaintiff side
(Kraft Heinz, Conagra, and Nestlé filed a follow-on suit against Tyson,
Pilgrim's Pride, and other processors in 2019 per Food Business News). This
does not change anything about Tyson's own entry, which was never in
dispute — flagging here only so Stage 4 doesn't accidentally conflate the
two companies' roles when writing both records from the same docket.

## 1. GAP step level

- **Claim:** Tyson not listed on GAP's Manufacturers directory page
  (checked 2026-07-30); a secondary/unverified claim attributed to Open
  Philanthropy Project says GAP "achieves its scale largely through
  contracts with large producers including Tyson Foods, Perdue Farms, and
  Whole Foods" — flagged as conflicting and unresolved.

  **DISPUTED — could not be resolved definitively in this pass; per the
  brief's instruction, this stays DISPUTED rather than defaulting either
  direction.** Independent WebSearch re-confirmed both sides exist:
  GAP's own Manufacturers and Farms & Ranches directory pages
  (globalanimalpartnership.org/partners/manufacturers/ and .../farms-ranches/)
  are real, current pages — the Manufacturers page does not list Tyson,
  consistent with Stage 2's direct fetch. Separately, the Open Philanthropy
  attribution recurred in this fact-check's own search results in
  substantively the same form Stage 2 found it, so it is a genuine claim
  (not a search-engine hallucination) — but it still was not traced to
  Open Philanthropy's original publication, and GAP's own 5-Step program
  structure (farms/ranches carry step ratings; a "manufacturer partner" is
  a separate designation for processors/brands that carry the GAP label on
  packaging) means the two claims are not necessarily contradictory: Tyson
  could plausibly *purchase from* GAP-rated farms without itself being a
  labeled "manufacturer partner." That reconciliation is plausible but
  UNCONFIRMED — it is a hypothesis, not a finding. **Verdict: DISPUTED,
  unresolved. Do not write that Tyson is or is not GAP-affiliated in any
  form; write only "not found on GAP's manufacturer-partner directory as of
  2026-07-30" if a GAP-related line is needed at all.**

- **Conclusion:** Stage 2's own conclusion ("NOT CHECKED exhaustively... do
  NOT write as absent") is UPHELD and reinforced by the DISPUTED status
  above being, if anything, a reason for more caution, not less.

## 4. Air-chilled vs. water-chilled (poultry)

- **Claim (flagged by Stage 2 as needing correction-check):** Tyson became
  an air-chilled chicken producer via acquisition of the Smart Chicken
  brand — sourced only from Smart Chicken's own marketing blog, unconfirmed
  from any other source in Stage 2's pass.

  **CORRECTED / UPGRADED to VERIFIED.** This fact-check independently found
  **Tyson's own investor-relations press release** confirming the
  acquisition: "Tyson Foods Acquires One of the Leading Brands of Organic
  Chicken," ir.tyson.com/news/news-details/2018/Tyson-Foods-Acquires-One-of-the-Leading-Brands-of-Organic-Chicken/,
  and the parallel release on tysonfoods.com/news/news-releases/2018/6/
  tyson-foods-acquires-one-leading-brands-organic-chicken (June 4, 2018) —
  Tyson acquired Tecumseh Poultry LLC, owner of the Smart Chicken brand,
  operated as a wholly-owned subsidiary. Independently corroborated by
  Lincoln Journal Star, The Shelby Report, Feedstuffs, and Talk Business &
  Politics, all reporting the same acquisition with consistent details
  (June 2018, Tecumseh NE-based, ~600 employees retained). Smart Chicken is
  air-chilled and organic — this is a real, current Tyson-owned brand.
  **Basis: VERIFIED (Tyson's own press release, company-disclosure, plus
  independent trade-press corroboration).**

  This does NOT mean Tyson's core commodity/Tyson-brand chicken is
  air-chilled — Stage 2's separate point stands and is UPHELD: the
  industry-standard/default method for Tyson-brand fresh chicken remains
  NOT CHECKED via direct Tyson statement. **Write these as two separate
  facts:** (1) Tyson owns the air-chilled Smart Chicken brand (VERIFIED),
  and (2) Tyson's flagship Tyson-brand chicken's chilling method is NOT
  CHECKED / not confirmed. Do not let (1) imply anything about (2).
