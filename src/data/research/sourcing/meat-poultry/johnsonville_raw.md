# Johnsonville (Johnsonville, LLC / Johnsonville Sausage) — raw sourcing research
_Researched: 2026-07-30 | Stage 2 researcher | RAW FACTS — not fact-checked_

**Overall finding for this company: thin public record.** Johnsonville is privately held, has no SEC filings (no 10-K/S-1 located, consistent with private status — the Stage 1 primary-source script's batch did not include a Johnsonville ticker/CIK), and — unlike Smithfield — does not appear to be a hog *producer/integrator* in the way the module's "gestation-crate status" and "vertical integration" claim types assume. It is primarily a sausage *manufacturer*. Several of the module's primary claim types (gestation-crate status, GAP step level, owned-vs-contract-farm sourcing) turned up little to no public disclosure. This is recorded as a genuine research gap, not evidence of good or bad practice.

## Sourcing model

**This is the weakest-documented section — flagged clearly rather than guessed.**

- No page was found on johnsonville.com or johnsonvillefoodservice.com disclosing whether the company's pork supply comes from company-owned farms, contract growers, or open-market purchasing (the "own flocks vs. contract" claim type the module asks for). **Searched, not found** — this is a real gap, not a confirmed "no disclosure exists anywhere" claim, since the company's site was not exhaustively crawled.
- One 2014-dated secondary trade-press figure states Johnsonville "had the most sow harvest capacity in the United States, with the facilities to harvest 3,400 pigs daily" — this describes *processing/harvest (slaughter) capacity*, not farm ownership or animal-raising practice, and is 12 years stale. Source attribution for this specific figure was not pinned to one dated article (surfaced in aggregate web-search synthesis). — UNVERIFIED, stale, and describes the wrong stage of the supply chain for a welfare/housing claim
- Johnsonville uses "DecisionNext" software for sourcing/harvest-planning/pricing analytics per vendor case studies — this is supply-chain *logistics* tooling, not a welfare or farm-ownership disclosure. — https://perishablenews.com/meatpoultry/decisionnext-announces-expanded-partnership-with-johnsonville/ ; https://consumergoods.com/johnsonville-sausage-ensures-demand-and-supply-chain-visibility — company/vendor self-disclosure, not independently verified, and not on-point for the housing/sourcing-model claim type
- **Conclusion for Stage 4:** Johnsonville's own-farm vs. contract-grower vs. open-market hog sourcing split should be recorded as `unknown` — no public source found in this pass, genuinely searched.
