## 2. Grass-fed vs. grain-finished (beef) — PRIORITY

- **Fact:** Tyson's own consumer-education blog post states: "All cattle are grass-fed for most of their lives. Cattle that spent their entire lives grazing on pastures – and never transported to a feed lot and given grain – is considered grass-finished." The same post does not claim any specific Tyson product is grass-finished.
  Source: Tyson Foods company blog, "How to Read Food Labels on Beef and Pork," https://thefeed.blog/2021/09/27/how-to-read-food-labels-on-beef-and-pork/ — fetched 2026-07-30, published 2021-09-27.
  Basis: company-disclosure (Tyson's own words, explaining terminology — not a claim that Tyson beef is grass-finished).

- **Fact:** Search of the American Grassfed Association's "AGA Approved Products" directory (americangrassfed.org/aga-approved-products/) did not surface Tyson or any Tyson-family brand. Direct WebFetch of that page returned HTTP 403 (blocked), so this is from search-engine synthesis only, not a direct page read.
  Basis: NOT CHECKED via direct fetch (403'd) — search-engine summary only. Do not write as confirmed absence.

- **Fact:** Tyson's beef sustainability program is "Progressive Beef" — a licensed quality-management system (food safety, antibiotic stewardship, environmental, animal welfare best-practices for feedlot operators), NOT a grass-fed/grass-finished certification. Tyson became the first protein company to license it in 2018; by 2020 Tyson had purchased 3M+ Progressive Beef-certified cattle, described as "more than half" of its cattle supply chain at that time.
  Source: Tyson Foods press release, "Tyson Foods Becomes First U.S. Food Company to Verify Sustainable Cattle Production Practices at Scale," https://www.tysonfoods.com/news/news-releases/2020/9/tyson-foods-becomes-first-us-food-company-verify-sustainable-cattle — 2020-09-09.
  Basis: company-disclosure (Tyson's own press release). Progressive Beef is a feedlot quality-management program, not a grass-fed/grass-finished welfare certification — do not conflate the two.

- **Fact — the misleading-claim finding (high priority for this brand):** Tyson launched "Brazen Beef" (Sept 2023, joint venture with Schweid & Sons) marketed as "climate-smart"/heading toward "net zero," including a USDA-approved "climate-friendly" claim (10% GHG reduction). Environmental Working Group (EWG) sued Tyson in 2024 alleging greenwashing — that Tyson never defined "climate-smart beef" or disclosed how GHG reductions were measured. Tyson settled in Nov 2025: agreed to stop making "net zero" and "climate-smart beef" claims and halt marketing/selling/promoting Brazen Beef in the U.S., for five years, and not make new environmental claims unless verified by a jointly-agreed expert.
  Sources (all secondary/news, cross-corroborating): EWG press release, https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news-release/2025/11/tyson-foods-agrees-stop-making-net-zero-and-climate-smart-beef — 2025-11; Earthjustice, https://earthjustice.org/press/2025/tyson-foods-agrees-to-stop-making-net-zero-and-climate-smart-beef-claims; Food Processing, https://www.foodprocessing.com/food-safety/environmental/news/55332752/tyson-agrees-to-drop-climate-smart-beef-and-net-zero-claims-in-settlement; case docket reference at Climate Case Chart, https://www.climatecasechart.com/collections/environmental-working-group-v-tyson-foods-inc-_4b986b.
  Basis: **UNVERIFIED** (multiple independent secondary/news sources agree on terms, but the underlying settlement agreement itself was not independently fetched by this researcher). Note for Stage 3: this is an environmental/climate marketing claim, not a direct animal-welfare claim, but it is squarely an "intentionally misleading claim" finding and Brazen Beef was marketed partly on a "grass-fed" angle per one source snippet (unconfirmed) — flag for follow-up whether Brazen Beef's grass-fed marketing was also part of the EWG complaint.

  Absent that acquisition claim, Tyson's core commodity chicken (Tyson-brand fresh chicken) is understood industry-wide to be water-chilled (standard method), but no Tyson-specific primary confirmation was found in this pass.
