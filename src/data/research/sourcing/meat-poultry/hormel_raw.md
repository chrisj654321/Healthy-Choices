## 2. Grass-fed vs. grain-finished (beef) — PRIORITY

- **Fact:** Hormel does not sell raw/fresh beef cuts as a mainline consumer brand (its portfolio is primarily pork, turkey, and processed/prepared foods: SPAM, Skippy, Justin's, Wholly, Columbus, Applegate, Jennie-O). The main beef-relevant product line found is **Applegate's beef hot dogs**.
  Basis: inferred from brand portfolio research in this pass; not a single cited source — flagging as reasoning, not fact-with-citation.

- **Fact:** As of a 2025 Hormel/Applegate press release, **100% of Applegate's beef hot dog portfolio is now sourced from "Certified Regenerative Beef"** — certified via Regenerative Organic Certified (ROC), Land to Market, and "Certified Regenerative" (endorsed by Certified Humane). This goal was announced and then reported as achieved ahead of a 2025 target.
  Source: Hormel Foods newsroom, "Applegate Farms, LLC Beats 2025 Regenerative Goal, Transitions All Beef Hot Dogs to 100% Certified Regenerative Beef," https://www.hormelfoods.com/newsroom/press-releases/applegate-farms-llc-beats-2025-regenerative-goal-transitions-all-beef-hot-dogs-to-100-certified-regenerative-beef/ — accessed 2026-07-30.
  Basis: company-disclosure (Hormel's own press release) — this is a real, named third-party certification (ROC / Land to Market / Certified Regenerative), but it is a **regenerative-agriculture / soil-practice certification, not a grass-fed/grass-finished certification**. **Important for Stage 4 write-up: do not conflate "regenerative" with "100% grass-fed and grass-finished."** No American Grassfed Association (or equivalent) certification was found for any Hormel-family beef product in this pass — this is exactly the kind of claim-category confusion the founder flagged as the top misleading-claim risk in this module.

## 3. Gestation-crate status (pork) — PRIORITY

- **Fact (specific, dated, primary-adjacent):** Per Hormel's own 2024 Global Impact Report (as summarized in trade coverage): **as of June 2024, 19% of total hogs Hormel Foods harvested (annualized basis) were produced in California Prop 12-compliant systems, and 13% were produced in loose (group) sow-housed systems** more broadly. Separately, **100% of hogs harvested specifically for the Massachusetts and California markets were housed in group pens from birth** (this is the legally-required subset for those two states' compliance regimes — Question 3 and Prop 12 — not a company-wide figure).
  Source: National Hog Farmer, coverage of Hormel Foods' 2024 Global Impact Report — accessed 2026-07-30 (Hormel's own report: https://www.hormelfoods.com/global-impact/global-impact-reporting/).
  Basis: UNVERIFIED (secondary trade-press summary of a company sustainability report — not independently re-fetched from Hormel's primary report document in this pass, but the report itself is a company-disclosure document, so even a direct read would still be basis=company-disclosure, not third-party-verified).

- **Fact:** Hormel states it committed to convert **company-owned** sow farms to group housing by 2018, and met that commitment early. Hormel purchases ~96% of hogs under supply contracts (as of FY2017, per a company 10-K reference) from 250+ independent producers, mostly Midwest family farms; the company also owns/operates some hog farms in Colorado.
  Source: search-engine synthesis citing a Hormel FY1999/2017-era 10-K and Hormel CSR materials — accessed 2026-07-30. **This figure (96%, FY2017) is STALE — nearly a decade old — and should NOT be presented as Hormel's current contract/ownership split without a current-year source.**
  Basis: UNVERIFIED / STALE (dated company-disclosure, not re-confirmed for current year).

- **Fact:** Applegate has required its pork suppliers to house sows in groups (not gestation crates) **since 1987**, per Hormel/Applegate's own materials.
  Source: search-engine synthesis of Hormel/Applegate press materials — accessed 2026-07-30.
  Basis: company-disclosure (Applegate/Hormel's own claim) — no independent third-party audit confirmation found for this specific historical claim beyond Applegate's general GAP/Certified Humane certifications noted in Section 1.
