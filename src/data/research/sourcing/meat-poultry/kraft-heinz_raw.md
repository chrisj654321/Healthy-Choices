## CRITICAL FLAG FOR STAGE 3 — resolve before writing any claim

Illinois, docket 1:16-cv-08637), alongside entities like Golden Tree Restaurants LLC, OSI Restaurant Partners LLC, Cheney Bros Inc., and McLane Company Inc. The Stage-2 brief characterized this as "Kraft Heinz Foods Company and Tyson Breeders, Inc. are BOTH named parties" without specifying role.

**This researcher followed up and found the docket's party list is NOT plaintiff/defendant-labeled — and the confirmed defendant/settling-producer list for this case (per a law-firm case tracker) is: Tyson, Pilgrim's Pride, Fieldale Farms, Peco, George's, Amick, Mar-Jac, Harrison Poultry, Simmons, Mountaire, O.K. Foods, HRF, Koch, Foster Farms, Perdue, Case, Claxton, Wayne Farms, Agri Stats, and Sanderson Farms — Kraft Heinz is NOT on that defendant list.**

The other names appearing alongside Kraft Heinz in the raw docket party list (restaurant chains, a foodservice distributor) are purchaser-side entities. Kraft Heinz Foods Company's real-world role is as a large industrial **purchaser** of raw chicken (it processes chicken into products), not a chicken producer. The overwhelmingly more plausible reading is that **Kraft Heinz Foods Company is a purchaser/plaintiff-class party in this litigation (alleging it was overcharged by the chicken-producer defendants), not a defendant accused of price-fixing.**

Source for defendant list: Lockridge Grindal Nauen PLLP case tracker, https://www.locklaw.com/litigations/broiler-chicken-antitrust-litigation/ — accessed 2026-07-30.

## 3. Gestation-crate status (pork) — PRIORITY (the strongest misleading-claim finding for this company)

- **Fact:** In 2012, then-Kraft Foods announced Oscar Mayer would move away from gestation stalls **by 2022**. This was one of several such pledges made 2012-2015 across the industry (Campbell's, Kraft Heinz, Arby's, Costco, etc.).
  Source: search-engine synthesis of Feedstuffs/thepigsite/WATTAgNet coverage — accessed 2026-07-30.
  Basis: UNVERIFIED (secondary/trade press, but a specific and widely-reported 2012 pledge).

- **Fact:** The 2022 target was **missed** — a "Quit Stalling" report (World Animal Protection, 2021) found most pork sellers, including companies with pledges like this one, were still failing to eliminate sow stalls.
  Source: World Animal Protection, https://www.worldanimalprotection.us/latest/news/quit-stalling-new-report-shows-companies-may-be-misleading-customers-false-promises-pig — accessed 2026-07-30.
  Basis: **UNVERIFIED and from an advocacy organization — this is a LEAD only, not established fact.** Per SKILL.md's rule, advocacy-group reports are recorded as secondary/leads, never as established fact, unless the underlying claim reached a court/agency action or company admission. Flag for Stage 3: does Kraft Heinz's own reporting corroborate the miss (see next entry) independent of the advocacy report?

- **Fact:** Kraft Heinz's own 2017 ESG report and 2018 proxy statement restated the commitment as "elimination of gestation stalls globally **by 2025**." The company's **2023 ESG Report language had softened to "phasing out the purchase of pork from suppliers who use gestation stalls"** — an ongoing/open-ended phrase rather than a completion date or percentage-achieved figure.
  Source: search-engine synthesis referencing Kraft Heinz's own 2023 ESG Report and 2018 proxy statement (SEC filing PX14A6G, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1637459/000121465924004993/d322243px14a6g.htm) — accessed 2026-07-30. Note: this SEC.gov PX14A6G URL was NOT independently fetched (WebFetch not attempted on it in this pass; other sec.gov Archives URLs in this same research session returned 403).
  Basis: company-disclosure (Kraft Heinz's own successive report language) — **this is the clearest documented instance in this batch of a company restating/softening a welfare commitment over time (2012: pledge by 2022 → 2018: restated as by 2025 → 2023: vague ongoing "phasing out" language, no completion percentage disclosed).** This pattern — firm date pledged, date passed, language softened — is exactly the kind of "intentionally misleading claim" pattern the founder asked this module to prioritize. No percentage-complete figure for Kraft Heinz's actual current gestation-stall-free sourcing was found in this pass (contrast with Hormel, which does publish a specific percentage — see hormel_raw.md).

- **Fact:** No GAP, Certified Humane, or AGW certification specific to Kraft Heinz's own gestation-crate-free status was found.
  Basis: NOT CHECKED exhaustively (directories' own search UIs not directly driven).

## 5. Stress/handling — GAP/Certified Humane audit scope

- **Fact:** Kraft Heinz's European Chicken Commitment pledge (see below) includes specific, named standards covering handling/slaughter: "controlled atmospheric stunning using inert gas or multi-phase systems, or effective electrical stunning without live inversion" — this is a specific, checkable handling/slaughter-method commitment, not a vague welfare claim.
  Source: Kraft Heinz UK site, https://www.kraftheinz.com/en-GB/animal-welfare-commitment — fetched directly 2026-07-30.
  Basis: company-disclosure (Kraft Heinz's own commitment page). **Scope is explicitly limited to Europe** (target: 100% of European chicken supply by 2026) — this does NOT apply to U.S. Oscar Mayer sourcing. Do not apply this European standard to U.S. product claims.

Per direct fetch of https://www.kraftheinz.com/en-GB/animal-welfare-commitment (2026-07-30), Kraft Heinz's pledge for European chicken supply by 2026 includes: compliance with all EU animal welfare laws regardless of production country; max stocking density 30kg/m² or less; higher-welfare breed genetics; minimum 50 lux lighting (incl. natural light); ≥2 metres of perch space and 2 pecking substrates per 1,000 birds; no cages/multi-tier systems; specified stunning methods (see above); third-party auditing with annual public reporting.

### USDA-FSIS enforcement / recalls

- **Fact:** On 2025-07-02, Kraft Heinz Foods Company recalled approximately **367,812 lbs** of fully cooked **Oscar Mayer Turkey Bacon Original** (12 oz vacuum packs, UPC 071871548601, "use by" dates 2025-07-18 through 2025-08-02, lot code RS40) due to possible **Listeria monocytogenes** contamination. Product was made 2025-04-24 through 2025-06-11; distributed nationwide plus exports to British Virgin Islands and Hong Kong. The contamination was discovered via Kraft Heinz's own internal lab testing; no illnesses reported as of the recall notice.
  Source: FSIS recall notice, title matched via search — "Kraft Heinz Foods Company Recalls Turkey Bacon Products Due to Possible Listeria Contamination," https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/kraft-heinz-foods-company-recalls-turkey-bacon-products-due-possible-listeria (direct WebFetch returned HTTP 403 — same tooling issue as sec.gov); ABC News and Food Dive coverage corroborate the same figures — accessed 2026-07-30.
