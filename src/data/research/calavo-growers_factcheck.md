## Issues

- **FDA Warning Letter — October 25, 2024** (CMS # 687785), addressed to Chantal Vos, Director of Hawaii Operations, Calavo Growers, Inc. Followed an FDA domestic investigation of the company's papaya packing/holding operation on April 16, 2024, and inspection of a contracted growing farm April 20–22, 2024. Findings: violation of the Produce Safety regulation (21 CFR Part 112) — the contracted farm did not provide adequate, readily accessible toilet facilities for workers in growing areas during harvesting; workers reportedly had to use nearby bushes because the sole toilet was located 0.30 miles from the harvest site. **This was a repeat finding** — the same type of violation was observed in a prior 2023 inspection at the same/similar site. Calavo representatives acknowledged the violations and verbally committed to relocating toilet facilities and adding composting toilets across contracted farms. This is an adjudicated FDA Warning Letter (formal compliance action), not merely alleged. Source: FDA, "Calavo Growers Inc 687785 10/25/2024," fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/calavo-growers-inc-687785-10252024 (accessed 2026-08-22); also reported by Food Safety News, "FDA cites Calavo Growers for repeat toilet facility violations," foodsafetynews.com/2024/12/fda-cites-calavo-growers-for-repeat-toilet-facility-violations-mena-food-group-for-food-safety-and-labeling-failures/ (Dec 2024). — **VERIFIED.** Direct WebFetch of the FDA.gov URL returned HTTP 404 this pass (likely dynamic rendering, same issue researcher hit), but WebSearch independently corroborated every specific detail: warning letter dated October 25, 2024, CMS #687785, April 16 domestic investigation + April 20–22 contracted-farm inspection, toilet facility 0.30 miles from harvest site, repeat finding from a 2023 inspection.
