### Civil litigation (class actions — "slack-fill" consumer/packaging claims; not a regulatory action, included because it names the US entity directly)
- **Kpakpoe-Awei v. Storck USA L.P.**, Case No. 18-1086, U.S. District Court, Southern District of New York (S.D.N.Y.), filed 2018-02-07. Plaintiff Jeffrey Kpakpoe-Awei alleged he overpaid for a 2.75-oz package of Werther's Sugar Free Caramel containing "excessive and non-functional" slack-fill (empty space) — complaint alleged the nontransparent 2.75-oz bags contained as much as 69% slack-fill, versus only 33% slack-fill in comparably-sized (5-oz) bags of the regular Chewy Caramels product line. **Outcome: settled** — U.S. District Judge Vincent L. Briccetti entered an order dismissing the case following a settlement between the parties (settlement terms/amount not found in this pass). Sources: [Top Class Actions — "Werther's Class Action Settlement Reached over Excessive Slack-Fill"](https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/lawsuit-news/werthers-class-action-settlement-reached-excessive-slack-fill/); [Law360 — "Werther's Caramels Maker Underfills Bags, Suit Says"](https://www.law360.com/articles/1010303/werther-s-caramels-maker-underfills-bags-suit-says) (paywalled, headline/snippet only).
