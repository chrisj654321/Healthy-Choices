### Civil litigation (alleged and adjudicated)
- **Class action re: artificial flavoring, Wise Cheddar & Sour Cream Ridgies:** Filed August 2020, alleging Wise failed to disclose on front-of-package that "cheddar" flavor is partially derived from artificial ingredients. **Dismissed** — judge ruled Wise's packaging representations were unlikely to mislead a reasonable consumer. Source: https://natlawreview.com/article/artificially-flavored-chips-case-dismissed (queried 2026-08-23).
- **Alce et al. v. Wise Foods, Inc.**, No. 17-02402, U.S. District Court, Southern District of New York. Plaintiffs Sameline Alce and Desiré Nugent alleged Wise systematically underfilled chip bags (slack-fill) — citing a 2.75-oz bag of Wise Ridgies Sour Cream and Onion chips filled to only 2.5 inches of its 10-inch capacity, and bags left 58%–75% empty against an alleged justifiable ~30% empty space. **Dismissed** March 27, 2018 — court ruled the slack-fill claim did not state a viable claim. Source: https://www.aol.com/article/finance/2017/04/05/wise-sued-for-allegedly-underfilling-potato-chip-bags/22027565; case outcome per https://www.classaction.org/news/lawsuit-consumers-paying-for-air-with-slack-filled-wise-foods-chip-bags (queried 2026-08-23).
