## Issues

- **Wrongful-death jury verdict — $241 million, February 27, 2026 (Madison County, IL circuit court):**
  - A Madison County, Illinois jury awarded $241 million ($49.5 million compensatory + $191.5 million punitive) to the family of Eric Johnson, a 64-year-old courier, over his 2016 death.
  - Facts: In 2016, Johnson picked up a load of frozen strawberries packed in dry ice at PFD Supply's facility in St. Charles, Missouri. He became unconscious while driving roughly 90 minutes later (carbon-dioxide off-gassing from the dry ice in an enclosed vehicle) and was found unconscious before leaving St. Charles County; he died three days later.
  - The court found Prairie Farms Dairy, Inc. (parent of PFD Supply) responsible for safety protocols at PFD Supply and found the company failed to implement/communicate a hazard-communication program under 29 CFR 1910.1200 covering dry-ice hazards — and that Prairie Farms had been cited for 1910.1200 violations both before this incident and multiple times since.
  - This is a jury verdict (adjudicated), though it may be subject to post-trial motions/appeal (appeal status not confirmed in this pass).
  - Source: Law360, "Prairie Farms Hit With $241M Verdict Over Dry Ice Death," law360.com/articles/2447253/prairie-farms-hit-with-241m-verdict-over-dry-ice-death; Legal Newsline, "Jury: Prairie Farms must pay Missouri family $241M over dry ice death," legalnewsline.com/madison-stclair-record/jury-prairie-farms-must-pay-missouri-family-241m-over-dry-ice-death/article_6f4a59f4-43aa-4b9d-96ed-8b554695b2c1.html; RiverBender.com, "Jury Awards $241 Million for Fatal Dry Ice Transport Incident" (all accessed 2026-08-22).

- **OSHA — fatality inspection, Birmingham, AL facility, 2020 (adjudicated, penalties paid):**
  - Facility: Prairie Farms Dairy, Inc., 36 Barber Court, Birmingham, AL 35209.
  - Fatality date: June 25, 2020 — an employee was "caught and killed in dairy palletizer equipment" after bypassing an interlocked safety gate door while attempting to clear a jam.
  - OSHA inspection opened June 26, 2020; citations issued December 1, 2020. **8 total violations: 6 Serious, 2 Other. Total penalty: $67,084.**
  - Itemized serious citations: 
    - 01001 — Std. 1910.22(a)(2), general workplace floor-condition hazard (separately detailed below) — $4,915
    - 01002 — Std. 1910.119(d)(3)(ii) — $8,193
    - 01003 — Std. 1910.147(c)(4)(ii) — $13,494
    - 01004 — Std. 1910.147(c)(5)(i) — $13,494
    - 01005A — Std. 1910.147(c)(6)(i) — $13,494
    - 01006A — Std. 1910.212(a)(1) — $13,494
    - Two "Other" citations (1910.37(a)(3), 1910.144(a)(1)(iii)) carried $0 penalty.
  - Note: Citation 01001 (a separate, earlier July 8, 2020 inspection at what appears to be the same or a related facility, standard 1910.22(a)(2)) describes employees in an Engine Room exposed to slipping hazards from standing water mixed with oil, and Dock-area workers exposed to chemical hazards from two leaking Wilden pumps containing Ecolab cleaning solution; 5 employees exposed; penalty $4,915; issued Dec 1, 2020; abatement completed by Dec 28, 2020.
  - Source: OSHA Establishment/Inspection Detail, osha.gov/ords/imis/establishment.inspection_detail?id=1480956.015 and osha.gov/ords/imis/establishment.violation_detail?id=1480956.015&citation_id=01001 (accessed 2026-08-22).
  - This same 2020 hazard-communication citation history (1910.1200) was cited as evidence in the 2026 wrongful-death trial above, per trial coverage — the plaintiffs alleged Prairie Farms had recurring 1910.1200 (Hazard Communication) citations before and after the 2016 death.
