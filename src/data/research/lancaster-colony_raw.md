## Issues

- **FDA — T. Marzetti Company (Lancaster Colony subsidiary) recalls:**
  - **March 2010:** Voluntary recall of T. Marzetti brand Veggie Dips (three varieties) and an Oak Lake Farms-brand chip dip due to potential Salmonella contamination traced to hydrolyzed vegetable protein supplied by a third party (Basic Food Flavors, Inc., Las Vegas, NV). Company stated no illness complaints received. Voluntary recall, not an FDA warning letter or adjudicated enforcement action. Source: Canada.ca recall notice referencing the FDA action, canada.ca/en/news/archive/2010/03/certain-t-marzetti-brand-veggie-dips-may-contain-salmonella-bacteria.html; original FDA recall notice URL referenced as fda.gov/Safety/Recalls/ucm202608.htm (accessed via search 2026-08-22; original FDA page no longer resolves).
  - **December 29, 2017 (posted to FDA Jan 2018):** Voluntary recall of frozen biscuit dough packed under multiple private-label brands (Southern Home, Shur Fine, Valu Time, Food Lion, SE Grocers, Piggly Wiggly, Lowes Foods, Premium Pick 5, Morning Fresh, Marshall's Old Fashioned, Laura Lynn) due to potential Listeria monocytogenes contamination. Distributed in AL, FL, GA, IN, KY, LA, NC, PA, SC, TN, TX, VA. No illnesses reported. Voluntary recall, not adjudicated. Source: FDA, "T. Marzetti Company Voluntarily Recalls Frozen Biscuit Dough Packed Under Various Brands Due to Potential Listeria Contamination," fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/t-marzetti-company-voluntarily-recalls-frozen-biscuit-dough-packed-under-various-brands-due (title/date confirmed via search index 2026-08-22; direct WebFetch of the page returned HTTP 404, likely due to dynamic rendering).
