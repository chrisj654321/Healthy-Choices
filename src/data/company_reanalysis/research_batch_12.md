- DB Status: New

- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2003-2010
- Details: Nicaraguan banana workers filed suit against Dole, Shell, Dow Chemical in Managua; initial judgment ordered $489.4 million payment (companies declined). - Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2009
- Details: Swedish filmmaker Fredrik Gertten produced documentary "Bananas!" about DBCP lawsuit. Dole filed defamation suit against filmmaker; Dole lost and ordered to pay filmmakers significant damages (in Swedish currency). Streisand effect amplified public awareness of supply-chain pesticide exposure.
- Source: Wikipedia, Dole plc; documentary records
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Ongoing Supply Chain Labor Risks

- Date: 2020-2025
- Details: Dole banana production in Central America continues in regions with documented labor rights vulnerabilities; ongoing exposure to wage/safety issues despite international scrutiny.
