## ADM (id: adm)

### Currently in DB
- Price Fixing Conviction (high): ADM paid $100M in 1996 for criminally fixing lysine and citric acid prices globally — one of the largest antitrust fines in US history at the time. (DOJ, 1996)
- HFCS Production (medium): ADM is a primary producer of high-fructose corn syrup and has lobbied extensively against sugar taxes and HFCS labeling requirements. (Center for Responsive Politics, 2022)

### Findings

#### Securities Fraud & Accounting Misconduct
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2026
- Details: SEC filed suit January 26, 2026, against former CFO Vikram Luthar for directing "adjustments" to Nutrition business unit transactions when segment missed 15-20% annual profit growth targets (FY2021-2022). Federal judge denied ADM's dismissal motions in 2026. CEO Juan Luciano, now-former CFO Luthar, former vice chairman Ray G. Young, and Vince Macciocchi (former nutrition president) all named in securities litigation.
- Source: SEC Filing, CFO Dive, Western Livestock Journal, 2026
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Ethanol Producer Antitrust Suit
- Status: Pending
- Date: 2024
- Details: Six ethanol companies from Iowa and Wisconsin filed anti-trust lawsuit against ADM alleging market manipulation and unfair competition in ethanol production supply chain.
- Source: Advanced BioFuels USA, 2024
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

## Cargill (id: cargill)

### Currently in DB
- Soy & Beef Deforestation (high): Cargill is repeatedly named as a top driver of Amazon and Cerrado deforestation through its Brazilian soy and beef operations. (Mighty Earth, 2022)
- Child Labor in Cocoa (high): Cargill and Archer Daniels Midland were defendants in a Supreme Court case (2021) over child labor in cocoa supply chains. (US Supreme Court, 2021)

### Findings

#### Supreme Court Child Labor Case - Dismissed on Jurisdictional Grounds
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2021
- Details: International Labor Rights Forum sued Cargill and Nestlé on behalf of six Malian children trafficked to Côte d'Ivoire, forced to work 12-14 hours daily with minimal pay/food and frequent beatings. US Supreme Court dismissed June 2021, ruling US courts lack jurisdiction for corporate complicity charges outside US territory.
- Source: International Labor Rights Forum / US Supreme Court, 2021
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Confirms existing issue

#### Washington D.C. Child Labor Lawsuit
- Status: Pending
- Date: 2023
- Details: Class action filed in Washington D.C. Superior Court alleging Cargill uses exploitative child labor on cocoa farms in Ghana. Case status ongoing.
- Source: Legal filings, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Updates "child_labor_cargill"

#### Brazil Labor Court Ruling on Child & Forced Labour
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2023
- Details: Brazilian Labour Court in Bahia found Cargill liable for child and forced labour in cocoa supply chain; ordered company to pay 600,000 reais (~USD 120,185) in compensation for collective moral damages.
- Source: Brazilian Labour Court, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Broken Deforestation Pledges
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2020
- Details: At UN Climate Summit 2014, Cargill CEO David MacLennan pledged to eliminate deforestation from supply chain by 2020. Rampant and illegal deforestation continued through the deadline and beyond, documented in multiple NGO audits.
- Source: Mighty Earth, 2022
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Confirms existing issue

#### OSHA Worker Safety Violations - Historical
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2012
- Details: OSHA cited Cargill Meat Solutions (Beardstown, IL) for three safety violations including exposing workers to hazardous energy from unexpected machine start-up without lockout/tagout procedures; $114,000 in proposed penalties for willful violations. Historical settlements include $400,000 OSHA payment for ergonomic and recordkeeping violations at Buena Vista CA, CalMo, and Jacksonville facilities related to occupational trauma disorders.
- Source: OSHA News Releases, Region 5, 2012
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Cumulative Penalty Record
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2000-2026
- Details: Cargill accumulated $263,976,899 in penalties since 2000 across 241 documented records for regulatory violations across multiple agencies.
- Source: Violation Tracker (Good Jobs First), 2026
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

## Bunge (id: bunge)

### Currently in DB
- Cerrado Deforestation via Soy (high): Bunge's Brazilian grain trading operations have been directly linked to soy sourced from illegally deforested Cerrado land. (Greenpeace Brazil, 2022)

### Findings

#### Cerrado Deforestation Scale & Ongoing Sourcing from Deforested Farms
- Status: Alleged
- Date: 2021-2023
- Details: Bunge exported 16 million tonnes soy from Cerrado (2018). In 2023, FoE reported Bunge purchased soy from farms responsible for 11,351 hectares deforestation post-2021. 95% of Bunge's deforestation risk concentrated in Cerrado. Bunge explicitly rejected 2020 Cerrado Manifesto commitment by major retailers (Tesco, Walmart, Unilever, McDonald's) to stop post-2020 sourcing; company stated acceptance of legally/risk-deforested soy until 2025.
- Source: Friends of the Earth US, Mighty Earth, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Confirms & Updates "brazil_deforestation_bunge"

#### Piauí Monopoly & 300% Deforestation Surge
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2021-2023
- Details: Bunge holds near-monopoly on soy trading in Brazilian state of Piauí; deforestation in areas linked to Bunge operations surged ~300% since 2021. Associates farms with insufficient due diligence.
- Source: Friends of the Earth US Report, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Indigenous Land Theft & Human Rights Abuses
- Status: Alleged
- Date: 2021-2023
- Details: Farm in Mato Grosso do Sul from which Bunge sources soy linked to land theft from Indigenous Guarani Kaiowá peoples. Human rights abuses documented in sourcing region.
- Source: Friends of the Earth US Report, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Harvard/TIAA Investment Connection
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2022
- Details: Friends of the Earth investigation revealed Bunge, Harvard University endowment, and pension fund TIAA jointly driving deforestation and land grabs in Brazilian Cerrado through coordinated soy production and land acquisition strategies.
- Source: Friends of the Earth US, ActionAid USA, 2022
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

## Barilla (id: barilla)

### Currently in DB
- Homophobic Statements Controversy (medium): Barilla's former chairman made homophobic statements in 2013 that sparked an international boycott; the company has since implemented LGBTQ+ inclusion programs. (Guardian, 2013)

### Findings

#### Supply Chain Disruption & Responsible Sourcing Decline
- Status: Alleged
- Date: 2021-2022
- Details: Ukraine war forced Barilla to change supply chains, making compliance with corporate responsibility guidelines difficult. Certified sustainable raw materials dropped from 70% (2021) to 67% (2022), primarily due to 300% decline in certified sunflower oil availability. Company unable to 100% certify sustainable sourcing amid geopolitical disruption.
- Source: Barilla Sustainability Report 2022, ESG Reporting, 2022
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Environmental Performance Gains
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2010-2024
- Details: Barilla achieved 32% GHG reduction and 24% water consumption decrease per ton finished product vs. 2010 baseline. Working with 9,000+ farms on sustainable/regenerative agriculture initiatives. ESG commitments documented in annual sustainability reports.
- Source: Barilla Group Sustainability Report, ESG Reporting, 2024
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: Confirms

## Kikkoman (id: kikkoman)

### Currently in DB
- High Sodium Soy Sauce (medium): Standard soy sauce contains ~900mg sodium per tablespoon, making it one of the highest-sodium condiments. Kikkoman has introduced low-sodium alternatives. (CSPI, 2022)

### Findings

#### Lead & Cadmium Labeling Requirement
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2020-2026
- Details: Kikkoman soy sauce carries California Prop 65 lead and cadmium warning labels. Heavy metals naturally occur in Japanese seaweed (kombu) used in soy sauce production; Japanese seaweed contains higher lead/cadmium levels than seaweed from other regions. Labeling reflects ingredient sourcing rather than manufacturing contamination incident.
- Source: Amazon/Consumer Q&A, California Prop 65, 2020-2026
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: Confirms existing issue

## Ajinomoto (id: ajinomoto)

### Currently in DB
- MSG "Chinese Restaurant Syndrome" Myth (low): Ajinomoto has actively campaigned to dispel debunked anti-MSG narratives and restore consumer trust in umami-based seasoning. (FDA, 2022)

### Findings

#### EPA Environmental Violations & Chemical Releases
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2024
- Details: EPA fined Ajinomoto Health & Nutrition North America Inc. $458,265 (December 2024) for violations of Clean Air Act and Emergency Planning & Community Right-to-Know Act (EPCRA) at Eddyville, Iowa facility. Violations included: hydrochloric acid release (2021) and chlorine gas release (2022) causing employee injuries. Required to install hazardous chemical containment protections.
- Source: US EPA News Release, 2024
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Major Glass Contamination Recall
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2026
- Details: Ajinomoto Foods North America expanded recall February 26, 2026, covering 36.9+ million pounds frozen food products (chicken fried rice, pork fried rice, ramen, shu mai) due to possible glass contamination. Products affected: October 21, 2024 – February 26, 2026 production window. USDA FSIS coordinated recall.
- Source: USDA FSIS Recalls & Alerts, Food Safety News, 2026
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Patent Infringement Litigation
- Status: Pending
- Date: 2025
- Details: Ajinomoto filed patent infringement lawsuits (October 2025) against Meihua Holdings Group Co. Ltd. (China) and FDJ Co. Ltd. (Japan) over alleged violations of Ajinomoto's patented MSG manufacturing processes.
- Source: Food Business Middle East & Africa, FoodBev Media, 2025
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: New

## JDE-Peets (id: jde-peets)

### Currently in DB
- Coffee Deforestation (medium): JDE's coffee sourcing regions — Brazil, Vietnam, Colombia — face ongoing deforestation pressure; the company's due diligence has been criticized as insufficient. (Global Canopy, 2022)

### Findings

#### Cerrado Deforestation Linked to JDE-Supplied Farms
- Status: Alleged
- Date: 2022-2025
- Details: Coffee Watch/AidEnvironment 2025 report documented 4,782 hectares cleared on six farms in Brazil's Cerrado since 2022 (including 299.5 hectares native vegetation), with farms selling green coffee beans to JDE Peet's and other companies. When cases shared with JDE in March 2025, company claimed deforestation precluded coffee harvesting before 2026, so farms "could not be linked to JDE operations." Deforestation continued through 2025.
- Source: Coffee Watch, AidEnvironment Report, 2025
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Updates "coffee_deforestation"

#### ILO Partnership on Child Labour Prevention (2023-Present)
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2023-2026
- Details: JDE Peet's launched FAIR COFFEE initiative with International Labour Organization as public-private partnership to combat child and forced labour in Brazil's coffee supply chain. Program prevented 40,000 hours of child labour and supported 900 children over three harvests (2023-2026). Demonstrates commitment to addressing supply chain labour risks alongside ongoing deforestation challenges.
- Source: DevDiscourse / ILO Press Release, JDE Peet's Sustainability, 2023-2026
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### EU Deforestation Regulation Compliance Scrutiny
- Status: Alleged
- Date: 2024-2025
- Details: Report by Coffee Watch and AidEnvironment found JDE Peet's potential non-compliance with EU Deforestation Regulation requirements. Company's due diligence and traceability claims questioned against documented ongoing deforestation in sourcing regions.
- Source: The Cooperator News, 2025
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New
