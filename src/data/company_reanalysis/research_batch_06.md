#### FTC Referral — Deceptive "Water" Advertising for Sparkling Ice
- Status: Adjudicated

- Status: Adjudicated (settled/dismissed)
- Date: 2018
- Details: A class-action lawsuit alleged that Talking Rain deceptively marketed Sparkling Ice fruit-flavored beverages as containing "natural flavors" when the products contained various additives. The suit drew attention to the gap between health-adjacent marketing and actual product composition.
- Source: TINA.org / Truthinadvertising.org, 2018; Food Safety News

#### Voluntary Recall — Sparkling Ice Cherry Limeade (Off-Taste/Off-Odor)
