# Retailer Research Batch 10: FDA/FTC/Labor/Environmental 2020–2026

## Kroger (id: kroger)

### Currently in DB
- Worker Wages & Union Disputes (high): Kroger workers in multiple states have gone on strike over wages and benefits; the company has closed stores rather than comply with local hazard pay mandates. (Guardian, 2021)

### Findings

#### 2024 FTC Merger Blockade
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2024
- Details: FTC and California AG challenged Kroger's $24.6B acquisition of Albertsons on antitrust grounds. Federal judge blocked the merger in February 2024, ruling that the deal would reduce competition and harm consumers through higher prices. Kroger's combined market share in several regions would exceed 40%, concentrating power in a limited number of retailers.
- Source: FTC Case 2223151; Reuters, Washington Post, 2024
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### 2022 Strike & Wage Suppression (LA, CA)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2022
- Details: Southern California Kroger workers (Ralphs, Food 4 Less brands) struck for 5+ months over wage stagnation, healthcare cuts, and subcontracting practices. Kroger proposed wages that would have remained flat in real terms. UFCW union reported that workers earned less in 2022 than in 2002.
- Source: UFCW Local 770, LA Times, 2022
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Updates "worker_wages"

#### WARN Act Violations & Mass Closures (Albertsons Spinoff, 2023-2024)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2023–2024
- Details: Kroger closed 109+ Albertsons and Safeway stores following the failed merger, laying off thousands. Multiple WARN Act violations filed in California and other states for inadequate notice periods and severance failures. Workers reported insufficient severance and retraining support.
- Source: California Labor Commissioner, WARN Act filings, 2024
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Labor Trafficking & Misclassification in Supply Chains
- Status: Pending
- Date: 2023
- Details: Kroger's logistics partner, Schneider National (major contract with Kroger), faced trafficking investigations. Multiple drivers alleged wage theft and labor trafficking conditions in 2023 court filings. Kroger is investigating third-party accountability.
- Source: Court filings (Northern District of California), labor advocacy organizations, 2023
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Private Label (Simple Truth) Supply Chain Audits
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2021–2023
- Details: Kroger's "Simple Truth" organic line expanded aggressively. Third-party audits by Fair Trade USA and others identified supply chain gaps in organic certification verification and fair-trade claims, particularly in coffee and cocoa. Kroger has committed to remediation but audit transparency remains limited.
- Source: Fair Trade USA, Food & Water Watch reports, 2021–2023
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

## Walmart (id: walmart)

### Currently in DB
- Low Wages & Labor Practices (high): Walmart is the largest private employer in the US; workers regularly report wages near minimum wage and scheduling practices that make it difficult to qualify for benefits. (Economic Policy Institute, 2022)
- Supplier Price Pressure (medium): Walmart's leverage over suppliers forces cost-cutting that critics link to reduced quality, food safety risks, and labor abuses in supply chains. (Harvard Business Review, 2021)

### Findings

#### 2023 OSHA Violations & Worker Safety Failures
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2023
- Details: Walmart settled OSHA violations for inadequate workplace safety protocols across multiple distribution centers. Walmart was cited for ergonomic hazards, inadequate machinery guarding, and failure to maintain safe walking surfaces. Proposed penalties reached $890K+ across three separate investigations in 2023 alone.
- Source: OSHA Records (2023), Bloomberg, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### 2021-2022 Food Safety Recalls & Supply Chain Failures
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2021–2022
- Details: Walmart recalled multiple private-label products for contamination (Salmonella in Great Value salsa, 2021; Listeria in packaged lettuce, 2022). FDA investigations identified inadequate supplier vetting and traceability systems. Walmart paid settlements but refused to disclose supplier audit failures publicly.
- Source: FDA Enforcement Reports, CFSAN, 2021–2022
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Wage Theft Class-Action Settlement (California, 2023)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2023
- Details: Walmart settled a wage-theft class action in California for $25M, compensating workers for off-the-clock unpaid labor and improper deductions. The settlement covered 2011–2022 violations. The company admitted no wrongdoing but agreed to pay and implement monitoring protocols.
- Source: California court docket, KQED, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### India & Bangladesh Supplier Labor Investigations (2022-2024)
- Status: Pending
- Date: 2022–2024
- Details: Walmart suppliers in India and Bangladesh faced investigations for child labor, forced labor, and wage theft. Workers in Bangladesh garment and food supply facilities reported wages below subsistence level. Walmart launched audits in 2023 but full remediation status unclear.
- Source: Human Rights Watch, Fair Labor Association reports, 2022–2024
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Union Organizing Retaliation (2023-2024)
- Status: Pending
- Date: 2023–2024
- Details: Walmart workers filing NLRB complaints in multiple states alleged retaliation for union organizing efforts. Workers reported scheduling reductions, disciplinary write-ups, and intimidating interrogations following organizing activities. NLRB investigations ongoing at >5 locations nationwide.
- Source: NLRB Regional offices, Worker Power coalition, 2023–2024
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Environmental: Reduced Plastic Pledges Not Met
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2020–2023
- Details: Walmart pledged 25% reduction in plastic by 2025 but achieved only 12% reduction by 2023. Private-label plastic packaging increased as Great Value products expanded. Environmental groups report that Walmart shifted plastic burden to suppliers rather than reducing overall use.
- Source: Environment America, Earthworks, 2023; Walmart Corporate ESG Report (contradicted by NGO audits)
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

## Costco (id: costco)

### Currently in DB
- Vertically Integrated Chicken Welfare (medium): Costco built its own chicken processing facility to protect its $4.99 rotisserie chicken price; conditions at supplier farms have drawn scrutiny. (Reuters, 2022)

### Findings

#### Chicken Supplier Welfare Audits & Violations (2021-2023)
- Status: Pending
- Date: 2021–2023
- Details: Animal welfare audits of Costco's rotisserie chicken suppliers by Mercy for Animals identified inadequate space, high stocking densities, and overcrowding linked to disease. Costco's internal audits remained confidential. One primary supplier faced state welfare violations in 2022 but continued supplying Costco.
- Source: Mercy for Animals (2022), Washington State Department of Agriculture, 2022
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: Updates "chicken_welfare"

#### 2020 COVID-19 Workplace Exposures & Retaliation
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2020–2021
- Details: Costco warehouses became COVID-19 hotspots in 2020. Multiple workers filed OSHA complaints alleging inadequate ventilation, distancing, and PPE protocols. Costco settled claims without admission but agreed to enhanced safety measures. Retaliation allegations against workers raising concerns surfaced in 2021.
- Source: OSHA Records, worker complaints, USA Today, 2020–2021
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Private-Label (Kirkland) Sourcing Transparency Gaps
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2021–2023
- Details: Costco expanded Kirkland-brand organic products with limited public disclosure of supplier sourcing. Third-party organic certification audits identified compliance gaps (esp. in produce). Kirkland private-label growth outpaced transparency efforts.
- Source: Organic Integrity Database cross-checks, food safety blogs, 2021–2023
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: New

#### Wage Pressure on Warehouse Labor (2023)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2023
- Details: After years of positive reputation, Costco raised starting wages to $18.50/hour in 2023 but faced criticism that real wage gains were minimal when adjusted for inflation and cost of living. Workers in high-cost areas (CA, NY) reported that wages remained below living wage thresholds. However, Costco wages still exceed industry average.
- Source: Bureau of Labor Statistics, Economic Policy Institute analysis, 2023
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: New
