## Land O'Lakes, Inc. (id: land-o-lakes)

### Currently in DB
- `fertilizer_runoff` (medium): Land O'Lakes' WinField United agribusiness unit sells fertilizers linked to nutrient runoff in the Mississippi River basin. Source: Environmental Defense Fund, 2021.

### Research Findings

#### FDA Warning Letter — Nutra Blend Subsidiary (Animal Feed Deaths)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2023 (warning letter May 22, 2023; inspection Dec 2022)
- Details: FDA inspected Nutra Blend LLC (a Land O'Lakes subsidiary, medicated feed mill in Mason City, IA) after reports of calf illnesses and deaths. The facility mistakenly added monensin (an ionophore antibiotic) into non-medicated cattle feed, killing multiple calves. The company failed to file a Reportable Food Registry report within the required timeframe, attempted to justify the delay by waiting for lab reanalysis, and had inadequate Preventive Controls Plans. Warning letter addressed to Lisa Deverell, President of Purina Animal Nutrition and EVP of Land O'Lakes.
- Source: https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/nutra-blend-llc-subsidiary-land-olakes-651174-05222023 (FDA, 2023); Penn State Food Safety News, Dec 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Antitrust Settlement — Dairy Price-Fixing (CWT Herd Retirement Program)
- Status: Adjudicated (settled 2016, conduct period 2003–2010)
- Date: 2016 settlement (conduct pre-2020, but settlement finalized in period of public record)
- Details: Land O'Lakes was a named defendant in a $52 million class-action antitrust settlement involving the Cooperatives Working Together (CWT) program. Plaintiffs alleged that between 2003 and 2010 more than 500,000 cows were prematurely slaughtered under CWT's dairy herd retirement program to artificially reduce milk supply and inflate prices. Land O'Lakes, Dairy Farmers of America, Agri-Mark, and Dairylea Cooperative were defendants. No admission of wrongdoing; settlement paid to dairy consumers in 15 states.
- Source: Hagens Berman press release, Sept 7, 2016; Dairy Reporter, 2016; https://www.hbsslaw.com/press/dairy-price-fixing/52-million-settlement-reached-over-dairy-industrys-nationwide-price-fixing-conspiracy-cow-killing
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Lobbying Spend — USDA, Congress, EPA
- Status: Public Record
- Date: Ongoing (latest available)
- Details: Land O'Lakes maintains an active federal lobbying presence via OpenSecrets (client ID D000022070), targeting USDA, Congress Agriculture Committee, and EPA. Reported spend of $1.2M/year in DB is consistent with OpenSecrets filings. Lobbying targets include dairy pricing policy, nutrition labeling, and agricultural environmental regulations.
- Source: OpenSecrets.org — https://www.opensecrets.org/federal-lobbying/clients/summary?id=D000022070
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: Confirms existing

#### WinField United — Fertilizer Runoff / Mississippi River Basin
- Status: Alleged / Ongoing Advocacy Issue
- Date: 2021–2024
- Details: WinField United (Land O'Lakes agribusiness subsidiary) is one of the largest US distributors of crop inputs including nitrogen fertilizers. Excess nitrogen and phosphorus from farms using WinField products has been linked by the Environmental Defense Fund and NRDC to hypoxic "dead zones" in the Gulf of Mexico fed by Mississippi River nutrient loading. No direct EPA enforcement action against Land O'Lakes specifically, but the systemic link is well documented.
- Source: Environmental Defense Fund, 2021; NRDC Mississippi River Report, 2022
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: Confirms existing (`fertilizer_runoff`)

## Organic Valley / CROPP Cooperative (id: organic-valley)

### Currently in DB
- `organic_standards` (low): As organic goes mainstream, Organic Valley has publicly opposed weakening of USDA organic pasture standards by large competitors. Source: NOSB, 2021.

### Research Findings

#### Lyons Magnus Co-Packer Recall — Microbial Contamination (2022)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2022
- Details: In July–August 2022, Organic Valley products manufactured at Lyons Magnus LLC's aseptic facility in Beloit, Wisconsin were recalled as part of a massive 35-million-package recall due to potential microbial contamination (Cronobacter sakazakii and Clostridium botulinum). FDA later issued a warning letter to Lyons Magnus (Jan 30, 2023) citing serious violations including failure to achieve commercial sterility, shipping ~37 batches of microbiologically contaminated product, and continuing production for months after becoming aware of contamination in April 2022. Organic Valley's branded single-serve aseptic milk products were among those recalled.
- Source: FDA Warning Letter to Lyons Magnus — https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/lyons-magnus-llc-645766-01302023; Food Safety News, Feb 2023; ABC7 Chicago, 2022
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Class Action Lawsuit — "Humanewashing" / Premature Calf Separation (2022–ongoing)
- Status: Pending (active litigation as of mid-2026)
- Date: Filed July 2022
- Details: PETA Foundation lawyers filed a class action (Takahashi-Mendoza v. Cooperative Regions of Organic Producer Pools, Case No. 22-cv-014564) alleging Organic Valley misled consumers about its animal care standards. The complaint alleges calves are prematurely separated from mothers and housed in isolated hutches, contrary to marketing claiming the "highest standards" of animal care. In 2023 a court held that a reasonable consumer could find Organic Valley's labeling misleading on calf separation practices, and allowed core claims to proceed. No settlement reached as of research date.
- Source: PETA.org, July 2022; Washington Post, July 19, 2022; classaction.org; Law360
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Organic Valley Grassmilk Product Recall — Microbial Sterility (2022)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: August 2022
- Details: Organic Valley issued a separate voluntary recall of 12/8oz aseptic single-serve Whole Grassmilk items due to potential microbial contamination and failure to meet commercial sterility specifications, as identified in preliminary root cause analysis. This recall was distinct from the Lyons Magnus recall above, though occurred in the same period.
- Source: Seward Community Co-op recall notice; FDA recall database, 2022
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Oregon Creamery Environmental Fine — Storm Drain Spill
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2019 (fine); 2021 (major fire at same facility)
- Details: The Organic Valley McMinnville, Oregon creamery was fined $26,574 by the Oregon Department of Environmental Quality for illegally dumping milk into a storm drain, turning a local creek white for three-quarters of a mile. In 2021, a major fire at the same McMinnville Creamery prompted evacuation within a half-mile radius due to ammonia presence; the plant was rebuilt and resumed operations in May 2022. The fine predates 2020 but the fire and rebuild fall squarely in scope.
- Source: Oregon DEQ records; local news coverage, 2019–2022
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: New

### Research Findings

#### Class Action — Phthalates in "Natural" Yogurt (2025–ongoing)
- Status: Pending (active as of June 2026)
- Date: Filed April 16, 2025 (S.D. Cal., Case No. 3:25-cv-00907-JES-VET)
- Details: An independent research organization found phthalates (endocrine-disrupting plastic chemicals) in Chobani Nonfat Plain Greek Yogurt and Whole Milk Plain Greek Yogurt in December 2024. Plaintiffs allege phthalates leached from polypropylene containers, yet containers and marketing state "only natural ingredients." Court partially denied Chobani's motion to dismiss in early 2026, allowing key claims to proceed. No recall issued; no FDA finding of unsafe levels. No settlement as of research date.
- Source: allaboutlawyer.com, 2026; topclassactions.com, 2025; rightfuladvice.com, 2026; Snopes fact-check, 2025
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### "Zero Sugar" Labeling Lawsuit — Dismissed (2025)
- Status: Adjudicated (dismissed)
- Date: Dismissed May 29, 2025
- Details: A class action alleged that Chobani Zero Sugar yogurt was misbranded under FDA labeling rules. Judge John J. Tharp, Jr. (N.D. Ill.) granted Chobani's motion to dismiss, finding the claims were preempted by federal FDA labeling regulations. No consumer payment; no product change required.
- Source: National Law Review, May 2025 — https://natlawreview.com/article/chobani-zero-sugar-lawsuit-dismissed-due-federal-preemption
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: New

#### Supply Chain Labor Concerns — Dairy Farmworkers
- Status: Alleged
- Date: 2021–2024
- Details: The Worker Justice Center of New York published an open letter to Chobani's CEO citing conditions at dairy farms in Chobani's supplier network: substandard housing, high injury rates, inadequate safety training, and documented retaliation against workers attempting to organize. At least one Chobani supplier was alleged to have fired farmworkers for organizing activity. Chobani responded by partnering with Fair Trade USA for supplier certification, but worker advocates criticized Fair Trade USA's track record on union rights.
- Source: Worker Justice Center of New York open letter — https://www.wjcny.org/chobani_open_letter; 2021–2022
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Voluntary Recall — Mold Contamination
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2013 (historical context); smaller localized action noted in 2020s
- Details: Chobani had a major mold-related recall in 2013 (pre-scope). More recently, Chobani has taken voluntary action with specific retailers to remove defective products related to dairy mold. No major FDA-classified recall found in 2020–2026 period.
- Source: Stewart Law Firm blog; FDA recall database cross-reference
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: New (minor note)

## Tillamook County Creamery Association (id: tillamook)

### Research Findings

#### Class Action — "Dairy Done Right" Marketing Deception (2019–ongoing; Oregon Supreme Court, 2025)
- Status: Pending (revived — Oregon Supreme Court ruled case has merit, April 2025)
- Date: Lawsuit filed 2019; Oregon Supreme Court ruling April 3, 2025
- Details: Consumer plaintiffs sued Tillamook alleging its "Dairy Done Right," "Goodbye Big Food," and pastoral imagery marketing campaigns are deceptive. The core allegation: up to 80% of Tillamook's milk comes from Threemile Canyon Farms in Boardman, Oregon — a 70,000-cow industrial CAFO visible from space — not the small coastal Oregon farms depicted in advertising. A circuit court dismissed in 2020; the Oregon Court of Appeals affirmed in 2022. On April 3, 2025, the Oregon Supreme Court reversed, holding the case has merit and allowing it to proceed to trial. Case is active as of June 2026.
- Source: OPB, April 3, 2025 — https://www.opb.org/article/2025/04/03/oregon-supreme-court-tillamook-lawsuit-case-moves-forward/; topclassactions.com; Corvallis Advocate, 2019
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Supplier Environmental Violations — Threemile Canyon Farms (Air, Water, Methane Credits Fraud)
- Status: Adjudicated (DEQ fine); Alleged (California credit fraud)
- Date: 2019–2022
- Details: Threemile Canyon Farms, Tillamook's primary milk supplier, was fined $19,500 by the Oregon DEQ for exceeding particulate matter pollution limits five times between June 2019 and September 2020. Food and Water Watch argued in 2022 that Threemile falsely certified environmental compliance while applying for California Low-Carbon Fuel Standard credits, meaning it collected clean-energy credits while violating air quality rules. Separately, a 2019 digester spill sent 300,000+ gallons of manure into the Tillamook River.
- Source: OPB, Jan 27, 2022 — https://www.opb.org/article/2022/01/27/dairy-methane-oregon-threemile-california-energy-credits/; East Oregonian, July 2022; Northwest Environmental Advocates
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### FDA Recall — Undeclared Allergens in Ice Cream (2023)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: May–June 2023
- Details: Tillamook voluntarily recalled a limited quantity of 1.5qt family-size Waffle Cone Swirl ice cream that had been mis-packaged in Chocolate Peanut Butter cartons, resulting in undeclared wheat and soy allergens. Up to 1,440 cartons affected, distributed only in Safeway stores in Washington state and northern Idaho. No illnesses reported.
- Source: FDA recall notice — https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/tillamook-issues-allergy-alert-undeclared-wheat-and-soy-tillamook-waffle-cone-swirl-ice-cream; KPTV, June 2023
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: New

#### FDA Recall — Foreign Plastic in Cheese (2024)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: May 2024
- Details: Tillamook voluntarily recalled over 1,000 cases of sliced Colby Jack and Monterey Jack cheeses due to possible presence of foreign plastic material. The FDA classified the recall as Class II (may cause temporary adverse health consequences but not likely to cause serious harm).
- Source: Newsweek, 2024; Grants Pass Tribune, 2024; FDA recall database
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Supply Chain — Mega-Dairy Groundwater Contamination
- Status: Public Record / Ongoing

Oregon's CAFO permitting framework requires nutrient management plans, but advocacy groups including NW Environmental Advocates document persistent failures to keep dairy manure out of Oregon waterways.
- Source: NW Environmental Advocates — https://northwestenvironmentaladvocates.org/staging/dairy-cow-manure/; OPB, May 2024

#### FDA Recall — Great Value Cottage Cheese (Pasteurization Failure, 2026)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: February 2026
- Details: Saputo Cheese USA voluntarily recalled select Great Value-brand cottage cheese products distributed to Walmart stores and distribution centers in 24 states (Feb 17–20, 2026) because liquid dairy ingredients may not have been fully pasteurized per California state regulatory standards. The issue was discovered during a pasteurizer troubleshooting exercise with the California Department of Food and Agriculture. Affected products were distributed to AL, AK, AZ, AR, CA, CO, GA, ID, IL, IA, KS, KY, LA, MO, MS, MT, NM, NV, OR, TN, TX, UT, WA, WY. No illnesses reported; pasteurizer subsequently repaired and certified.

- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2022–2023
- Details: Saputo Inc. recalled various Gouda products produced at its Green Bay, Wisconsin facility — including Great Midwest Applewood Smoked Gouda and Dutchmark Smoked Gouda — after supplier Deutsch Kase Haus notified Saputo that testing showed potential Listeria monocytogenes contamination in cheese it had provided.

#### Canadian Trade Policy — Class 7 Pricing Dispute
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2018–2022

This is a regulatory/lobbying matter rather than an enforcement action, but illustrates Saputo's political posture on dairy pricing.

No formal EPA or state enforcement action found for the 2020–2026 period, but the pattern of community pushback on water use is documented.

## Vital Farms, Inc. (id: vital-farms)

### Research Findings

#### PETA/Consumer Lawsuit — "Humanewashing" / Pasture-Raised Claims (2021–2025)
- Status: Adjudicated (dismissed January 2025, Vital Farms prevailed)
- Date: Filed May 2021; dismissed January 2025
- Details: PETA Foundation lawyers co-filed a class action alleging Vital Farms misled consumers via "ethical," "certified humane," and "pasture-raised" marketing. Specific allegations: (1) hatcheries in Vital Farms' network kill all male chicks at birth; (2) beak-tipping (debeaking) of hens; (3) hens spend most time indoors rather than on pasture; (4) spent hens sold to pet food companies for slaughter. In December 2023, Vital Farms was ordered to pay $292,000 in sanctions to PETA for discovery abuse (not a consumer settlement). PETA dropped out in May 2023. The class action was formally dismissed in January 2025 with no consumer payment and no settlement. Vital Farms prevailed.
- Source: Vital Farms response page — https://vitalfarms.com/whats-happening-with-the-vital-farms-lawsuit/; PETA.org; allaboutlawyer.com, 2026; Green Matters
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Listeria-Linked Recall — Hard-Boiled Eggs (Almark Foods, 2019–2020)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: December 2019 / expanded January 2020
- Details: The FDA recalled hard-boiled egg products from Almark Foods' Georgia facility due to potential Listeria monocytogenes contamination, including three varieties of Vital Farms pasture-raised hard-cooked eggs. The broader Almark outbreak was linked to 7 illnesses, 4 hospitalizations, and 1 death across 5 states; approximately 276,000 pounds of egg products recalled. Vital Farms was a brand affected, not the source facility.
- Source: FDA recall notice, Dec 2019–Jan 2020; Schmidt & Clark law (case background)
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### 2026 Social Media Controversy — Linoleic Acid in Eggs
- Status: Alleged / Ongoing public debate
- Date: January 2026
- Details: A study by Nourish Food Club and Michigan State University found that "organic pasture-raised" Vital Farms eggs contained linoleic acid at 22.5% of fatty acid composition — higher than a tablespoon of canola oil. Critics on social media argued this undermines the premium health positioning of pasture-raised eggs, potentially because of supplemental feed composition. No new lawsuit filed as of June 2026, but the viral controversy has renewed scrutiny of the company's premium price claims.
- Source: allaboutlawyer.com, Feb 2026; Parade, 2026 — https://parade.com/food/vital-farms-egg-boycott-greenwashing-claims-january-2026
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: New

### Research Findings

#### Vermont State Environmental Fine — Wastewater Permit Violations (2026)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: February 2026 (violations ongoing since July 2022)
- Details: The Vermont Agency of Natural Resources fined Agri-Mark, Inc. (d/b/a Cabot Creamery) $60,000 for multiple violations of its Pretreatment Discharge Permit at its Middlebury, Vermont dairy and whey processing facility. Since July 1, 2022, Agri-Mark committed 15 permit violations including: repeated exceedances of Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD) and pH effluent limits; unpermitted discharges of high-strength dairy waste to the municipal wastewater treatment system; multiple monitoring and reporting failures; and failure to maintain the facility in good working order with adequate staffing. The violations caused measurable impacts on Middlebury's municipal wastewater treatment plant. The consent order was entered by the Vermont Superior Court Environmental Division. Note: Vermont taxpayers had funded a $716,000 upgrade to the Middlebury facility, making the ongoing violations especially controversial.
- Source: Vermont Agency of Natural Resources press release — https://anr.vermont.gov/news/agri-mark-inc-fined-multiple-wastewater-permit-violations-their-vermont-facility; Seven Days VT, Feb 2026; WCAX, Feb 14, 2026; Vermont Daily Chronicle, 2026

#### Voluntary Recall — Listeria Contamination from Rizo-Lopez Supplier (2024)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: February 5, 2024
- Details: Sargento voluntarily recalled products containing Cotija cheese supplied by Rizo-Lopez Foods Inc. - Source: FDA outbreak investigation — https://www.fda.gov/food/outbreaks-foodborne-illness/outbreak-investigation-listeria-monocytogenes-queso-fresco-and-cotija-cheese-february-2024; NBC News; CBS News; Food Safety News, March 2024
