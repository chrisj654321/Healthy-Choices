## VERIFICATION METHODOLOGY

Each finding was searched against primary sources (FDA databases, OSHA/DOL announcements, court records, FTC filings, ASA rulings, SEC filings) and credible trade/investigative journalism. Verdicts are assigned per the standard rubric:

- ✅ VERIFIED — confirmed via primary source (court record, FDA/OSHA database, FEC filing, official press release)
- ⚠️ ALLEGED — credible media reports but not adjudicated or outcome not fully public
- 📅 OUTDATED — fully resolved >5 years ago with no ongoing relevance to current consumers
- ❌ REMOVE — speculative, no credible source, involves private individuals, unacceptable liability, or date error that makes the finding factually wrong
