## Kroger (kroger)

Oregon, Judge Adrienne Nelson) granted preliminary injunction halting the ~$24.6B Albertsons acquisition on **Dec 10, 2024** (research said Feb 2024 — corrected to Dec 2024). NOT confirmed: a "5+ month strike" or "workers earned less in 2022 than 2002." Reframe as wage dispute / strike authorization. Hedge.
- **WARN Act violations / 109 store closures** — ⚠️ ALLEGED. Store divestiture/closure plans tied to the failed merger are documented, but specific adjudicated WARN Act violations in CA are not confirmed via primary source. Hedge heavily or drop the "violation" framing. Keep as MEDIUM, hedged.
- **Labor trafficking (Schneider National) in supply chain** — ❌ REMOVE. No credible primary source ties Kroger to Schneider trafficking findings; speculative and high-liability. Remove.
- **Simple Truth audit gaps** — ⚠️ ALLEGED, low confidence. Generic NGO-report framing, no specific finding. Drop or fold into a soft transparency note. LOW.
- **Fair Food Program holdout** — ✅ VERIFIED (cross-found). CIW documents Kroger as a Fair Food Program holdout alongside Publix. Strong, citable. Add this in place of the trafficking claim.
