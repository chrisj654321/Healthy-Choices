## Nestlé S.A. (id: nestle)

### Currently in DB
- **water** (high): Water Privatization — lobbied against public water regulations, extracted water from drought-stressed communities at below-market rates. (Mother Jones, 2021)
- **infant** (high): Infant Formula Marketing — repeatedly violated WHO breast-milk substitute marketing standards in developing countries. (WHO / IBFAN Report, 2022)
- **child_labor** (high): Child Labor in Cocoa Supply Chain — documented use of child labor in Ivory Coast cocoa farms. (Washington Post Investigation, 2019)

### Research Findings

#### Buitoni Pizza E. Coli Outbreak — Criminal Charges
- Status: Adjudicated (criminal charges filed; proceedings ongoing)
- Date: 2022 (charges 2024)
- Details: France's largest-ever E. coli HUS outbreak linked to Nestlé's Buitoni Fraîch'Up frozen pizzas. 56 confirmed cases, 2 children dead, 50 cases of Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome. French judges placed Nestlé France and subsidiary SPAC under formal criminal investigation for involuntary manslaughter, deceitful practices, and endangering others. Nestlé closed the Caudry plant in 2023 and reached compensation settlements with 63 victims. Criminal trial pending as of mid-2026.
- Source: Food Safety News, 2024; Euronews, 2022; Marler Blog, 2024
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Infant Formula Global Recall — Cereulide Toxin (2025–2026)
- Status: Public Record / Pending (criminal investigation opened)
- Date: 2025–2026
- Details: Nestlé's largest-ever recall, covering 800+ products from 10+ factories in 60+ countries. Bacillus cereus detected at a Dutch factory on November 26, 2025; Nestlé delayed notifying Dutch food authorities until December 9 — a 13-day gap that triggered a criminal probe. Root cause traced to a contaminated ARA (arachidonic acid) oil ingredient from a Chinese supplier. At least 7 European nations reported infants with GI distress. Austrian health ministry called it "the largest recall campaign in the company's history." IBFAN cited the crisis as further evidence that Nestlé's infant formula credibility is fundamentally compromised.
- Source: Food Safety News, 2026; Pharmacally, 2026; IBTimes, 2026; Euronews, 2026; IBFAN, 2026
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Cocoa Child Labor — Ongoing Federal Litigation
- Status: Pending (active litigation)
- Date: 2021–present
- Details: International Rights Advocates filed a class action (Coubaly v. Nestlé et al.) on behalf of former child laborers from Mali trafficked to Ivory Coast cocoa farms. The U.S. Supreme Court in 2021 dismissed an earlier case on personal jurisdiction grounds, but new suits continue. Corporate Accountability Lab's September 2023 report documented ongoing child labor on farms supplying Nestlé, Mars, Ferrero, and Barry Callebaut. Nestlé's own "Child Labor Monitoring and Remediation System" covers only a fraction of farms in its supply chain, per independent audits.
- Source: International Rights Advocates (IRAadvocates.org); Corporate Accountability Lab, 2023; Harvard Law Systemic Justice Project, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Confirms existing (existing entry is not outdated; new litigation adds urgency)

#### Nestlé Toll House Cookie Dough Recall — Wood Contamination
- Status: Adjudicated (voluntary recall completed)
- Date: 2023 & 2025
- Details: August 2023: voluntary recall of Nestlé Toll House Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough Bar (16.5 oz) for potential wood fragments. March 2025: second frozen meal recall for wood contamination in products produced August 2024–March 2025, following consumer injury reports.
- Source: FDA.gov recall notices, 2023; TopClassActions, 2025
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Perrier "Natural" Mineral Water Class Action
- Status: Pending (class action)
- Date: 2024
- Details: Proposed class action filed March 2024 alleging Nestlé USA misled consumers about the purity and natural qualities of Perrier, claiming the water undergoes processing steps that strip its "natural mineral water" designation under applicable standards.
- Source: ClassAction.org, 2024
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Lobbying Against Child Labor Legislation
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2020–2024
- Details: When the U.S. Senate considered bills strengthening child labor import restrictions on cocoa, Nestlé (alongside Mars, Hershey, and Cargill) funded an intensive lobbying campaign that stalled the legislation. Companies have spent millions on lawyers, lobbyists, and PR firms to resist accountability rather than address supply chain conditions. The industry made pledges to eliminate the worst forms of child labor by 2005, then 2008, then 2010, then 2025 — each deadline missed.
- Source: Rights Action; Harvard Law Systemic Justice Project, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

## PepsiCo, Inc. (id: pepsico)

### Currently in DB
- **soda_tax** (medium): Soda Tax Opposition — spent millions lobbying against soda taxes and sugar warning labels. (CSPI, 2023)
- **plastic** (high): Plastic Pollution — one of the world's top plastic polluters; consistently appears in ocean plastic audits. (Break Free From Plastic, 2023)

### Research Findings

#### FTC Robinson-Patman Price Discrimination Lawsuit
- Status: Adjudicated (dismissed without prejudice, May 2025; details later unsealed)
- Date: 2025
- Details: In January 2025, the FTC sued PepsiCo under the Robinson-Patman Act, alleging that PepsiCo gave Walmart illegal preferential pricing — lower average retail prices, promotional payments, and supply chain support not offered proportionally to competing retailers. PepsiCo allegedly tracked retail prices across the market to ensure no competitor undersold Walmart on Pepsi products, and raised wholesale prices to non-Walmart retailers to fund Walmart-only discounts. The FTC dismissed the suit in May 2025 under the new administration, but in December 2025 a federal judge ordered the previously-sealed complaint details to be made public, fully exposing the alleged scheme. Senator Elizabeth Warren pressed the FTC on the dismissal. Independent analysts at The American Prospect described the revealed documents as exposing a systematic "monopolization machine."
- Source: FTC.gov, 2025; NPR, January 2025; Grocery Dive, 2025; American Prospect, December 2025; Bloomberg Law, 2025
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Plastic Pollution Lawsuits — Multiple State AGs
- Status: Pending (multiple active lawsuits)
- Date: 2023–2025
- Details: New York AG filed suit against PepsiCo in November 2023 over plastic pollution in the Buffalo River, alleging public nuisance from microplastic fragments derived from Pepsi packaging. California filed a similar suit (Superior Court of CA, County of LA, Case No. 24STCV28450) in 2024. Los Angeles County filed suit in October 2024 against both PepsiCo and Coca-Cola alleging they deceived the public about plastics' recyclability while knowing the infrastructure to recycle most plastics doesn't exist. Baltimore filed a parallel lawsuit in June 2024. A July 2024 court order allowed Earth Island Institute's landmark pollution nuisance suit against PepsiCo, Coca-Cola, and P&G to proceed.
- Source: Top Class Actions, 2023; Fortune, November 2024; Plastic Pollution Coalition, July 2024; LA County press release, October 2024
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Updates "plastic" (ongoing litigation escalates existing entry)

#### Quaker Oats Salmonella Recall
- Status: Adjudicated (recall complete; class action filed)
- Date: 2023–2024
- Details: PepsiCo's Quaker Oats subsidiary recalled multiple granola bar and granola product SKUs in late 2023 and expanded the recall in early 2024 due to potential Salmonella contamination. Dozens of products under the Quaker brand were affected, including popular granola bars marketed to children. A class action was subsequently filed.
- Source: FDA.gov; Food Safety News, 2023–2024
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Soda Tax Lobbying — Documented Multi-State Campaign
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2020–2025
- Details: PepsiCo continued aggressive lobbying against sugar-sweetened beverage taxes in multiple states and cities, including coordinated industry campaigns through the American Beverage Association. CSPI documented millions in state-level spending. In some jurisdictions, PepsiCo funded deceptive "community coalition" front groups to oppose taxes while obscuring corporate backing.
- Source: CSPI, 2023; Center for Responsive Politics (OpenSecrets)
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: Confirms existing

## Kellanova / Kellogg's (id: kelloggs)

### Currently in DB
- **labeling** (medium): GMO Labeling Opposition — spent heavily to oppose mandatory GMO labeling in California and Washington. (Open Secrets, 2012–2014)

### Research Findings

#### 2021 BCTGM Strike — Unfair Labor Practices Allegations
- Status: Adjudicated (strike resolved December 2021; related NLRB complaints)
- Date: 2021
- Details: Roughly 1,400 Kellogg's cereal workers (Bakery, Confectionery, Tobacco Workers and Grain Millers union — BCTGM) struck from October 5 to December 21, 2021, at plants in Battle Creek MI, Omaha NE, Lancaster PA, and Memphis TN. Core disputes: a two-tier wage system paying new hires ~$22/hr vs. legacy workers ~$35/hr, healthcare, retirement benefits, and mandatory overtime of up to 80 hours/week. During the strike, Kellogg's filed suit against the Omaha BCTGM local; labor advocates characterized the suit as an intimidation tactic. The strike was the largest in the food industry that year. A contract was ratified December 21, 2021. Workers alleged the two-tier system violated parity commitments; Rolling Stone documented 80-hour work weeks as "standard."
- Source: NPR, December 2021; Rolling Stone, 2021; Business & Human Rights Resource Centre, 2021; Wikipedia
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Mars Acquisition of Kellanova — $35.9B Deal Completed
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2024–2025
- Details: Mars, Inc. announced acquisition of Kellanova (formerly Kellogg Company) on August 14, 2024, for $35.9 billion. Kellanova shareholders approved November 1, 2024. FTC cleared the deal June 26, 2025. Transaction closed December 11, 2025. Kellanova's brands (Pringles, Pop-Tarts, Cheez-It, Eggo, MorningStar Farms, Nutri-Grain) now sit under Mars's ownership. The consolidation concentrates significant snack-food market power.
- Source: Food Business News, 2025; Mars.com press release; Convenience Store News, 2025
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Honey Smacks Salmonella — Supplier Criminal Conviction
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2018–2022 (resolution)
- Details: 2018 Salmonella outbreak linked to Kellogg's Honey Smacks cereal (produced at Kerry Inc.'s Illinois plant) sickened 135 people across 36 states. FDA issued a warning letter to Kerry Inc. for severe CGMP violations. Kerry Inc.'s former Quality Assurance Director pleaded guilty to a federal misdemeanor. The plant was permanently closed. Although the core events predate the 2020 focus period, this matter was not resolved until after 2020 and remains a significant safety incident in Kellogg's history.
- Source: National Law Review; FDA Warning Letter; Marler Blog
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### GMO Labeling — Outdated DB Entry
- Status: Public Record
- Date: DB entry cites 2012–2014 activity
- Details: The existing DB issue (id: "labeling") covers activity from 2012–2014. Mandatory GMO disclosure became federal law under USDA's National Bioengineered Food Disclosure Standard (effective 2022). The issue of Kellogg's opposition to GMO labeling is therefore largely resolved and the DB entry is stale.
- Source: USDA NBFDS, 2022
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: Existing is outdated — recommend downgrading severity and updating description

## General Mills, Inc. (id: general-mills)

### Currently in DB
- **glyphosate** (high): Glyphosate in Oat Products — independent testing found glyphosate residues above EWG thresholds in many oat-based products. (EWG Report, 2022)

### Research Findings

#### Gold Medal Flour — Recurring Salmonella Recalls (2023)
- Status: Adjudicated (recalls complete; FDA outbreak investigation closed)
- Date: 2023
- Details: On April 28, 2023, General Mills recalled 2-, 5-, and 10-pound bags of Gold Medal Unbleached and Bleached All Purpose Flour (best by dates March 27–28, 2024) due to potential Salmonella Infantis. FDA/CDC linked the recall to a confirmed multistate outbreak spanning at least 11 states / 13 patients. A second recall followed in September 2023 for 5-pound bags of Gold Medal Unbleached flour with separate best-by dates, after sampling showed potential E. coli O121 contamination. This is at least the third General Mills flour recall in five years (previous: 2019, 2020 E. coli outbreaks).
- Source: FDA.gov Outbreak Investigation, April 2023; Food Safety News, May 2023; NBC News, 2023; Top Class Actions, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Nature Valley "Natural" Labeling — Settlement and Label Change
- Status: Adjudicated (settled)
- Date: 2016–2018 (resolved), confirmed with label update 2025
- Details: General Mills settled a lawsuit filed by consumer groups (Organic Consumers Association, Moms Across America, Beyond Pesticides) alleging that Nature Valley granola bars and related oat products were deceptively labeled "100% Natural" while containing glyphosate residues. General Mills agreed to drop the "100% Natural" claim without admitting wrongdoing. As of 2025, an Insurance Journal report confirmed the label change has been implemented across all Nature Valley product lines. Glyphosate was detected at 0.45 ppm in independent testing — below the EPA limit (30 ppm) but above EWG's health benchmark of 0.01 ppm.
- Source: Food Dive, 2018; Bakery & Snacks, 2018; Insurance Journal, July 2025; NaturalNews, 2017; AboutLawsuits.com
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Confirms and contextualizes existing "glyphosate" entry; settlement and label update should be noted

#### FDA "Healthy" Label Dispute — Cereal Industry Pushback
- Status: Public Record / Pending (regulatory)
- Date: 2022–2024
- Details: General Mills joined Kellogg's and Post Consumer Brands in threatening legal action against FDA over proposed updates to the definition of "healthy" for food labeling. The companies argued that FDA's draft rule would harm cereals by requiring them to reduce sodium and/or sugar to qualify for the "healthy" label, even if the overall nutritional profile is positive. This illustrates industry lobbying against stronger label standards.
- Source: Just Food / Food Dive, 2022
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Ongoing Glyphosate Contamination — Industry-Wide
- Status: Pending (continued testing / litigation risk)
- Date: 2022–2025
- Details: EWG's 2022 report found glyphosate in a majority of oat-based General Mills products tested, including Cheerios and other cereals, at levels above EWG's children's health benchmark. General Mills has not committed to eliminating glyphosate residues or transitioning to organic oat sources. Follow-up litigation risk remains elevated given public health advocacy attention.
- Source: EWG Report, 2022
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Confirms existing

## Unilever PLC (id: unilever)

### Currently in DB
- **palm_oil** (high): Palm Oil Deforestation — supply chain linked to deforestation and human rights abuses in Southeast Asia despite pledges. (Rainforest Action Network, 2023)

### Research Findings

#### Dry Shampoo Benzene Recall and Class Action Settlement
- Status: Adjudicated (recall complete; class action settlement pending final approval)
- Date: 2022–2025
- Details: In October 2022, Unilever recalled a wide range of aerosol dry shampoos (Dove, Nexxus, Suave, TIGI Bed Head/Rockaholic, TRESemmé) after testing revealed elevated levels of benzene, a known human carcinogen. Class action lawsuits were filed nationwide. In early 2025, a proposed $3.625 million settlement was submitted for preliminary court approval, but a federal judge denied it — finding the proposed class was too broad and some class members lacked Article III standing. The litigation continues.
- Source: TopClassActions.com, 2025; ClassAction.org ($3.6M settlement report); MedTruth; Forensis Group
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Ben & Jerry's vs. Unilever — Censorship and CEO Termination Lawsuits
- Status: Pending (active litigation)
- Date: 2022–2025
- Details: Ben & Jerry's sued Unilever in July 2022 over the unauthorized sale of Ben & Jerry's Israeli business to a local licensee, claiming a violation of the 2000 merger agreement. The parties settled in December 2022, with Unilever paying $5 million to Ben & Jerry's ($2.5M in 2023, $2.5M in 2024). However, the conflict reignited in November 2024 when Ben & Jerry's and five independent board members sued Unilever and subsidiary Conopco in federal court, alleging that Unilever censored at least four social media posts related to Gaza/Palestine between 2023–2024, violating the merger agreement's social mission protections. In March 2025, Ben & Jerry's filed additional claims alleging Unilever fired the company's CEO in retaliation for his political/activist positions, without following the merger agreement's required advisory process.
- Source: CBS News, 2025; NPR, March 2025; CNBC, 2022; ESG Dive, 2024; Unilever press release, December 2022
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Nutrafol FTC/FDA Complaint — Deceptive Hair Loss Claims
- Status: Alleged (regulator complaint filed; no formal enforcement action announced)
- Date: 2023
- Details: TINA.org (Truth in Advertising) filed a complaint with the FTC and FDA in 2023 alleging that Unilever's Nutrafol brand marketed supplements as "medical-grade" and "clinically proven" to increase hair growth and prevent hair loss in violation of FTC and FDA regulations. Nutrafol's advertising appeared on websites, social media, Amazon, and national TV. No formal FTC or FDA enforcement action was publicly announced, but the complaint put the brand under regulatory scrutiny.
- Source: Yahoo Finance / TINA.org, 2023
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Palm Oil Deforestation — Persistent Supply Chain Failures
- Status: Public Record / Alleged
- Date: 2022–2024
- Details: Despite Unilever's long-standing pledge to achieve a deforestation-free supply chain by 2023, Rainforest Action Network's 2023 report documented ongoing linkages between Unilever's palm oil suppliers and deforestation in Indonesia and Malaysia, as well as human rights violations against local communities. In 2022, Mondelēz competitor reporting also implicated common suppliers (e.g., Agropalma) in forced displacement and community conflict in Brazil — issues that have parallels in Unilever's supply base. Unilever officially missed its 2023 deforestation-free palm oil deadline.
- Source: Rainforest Action Network, 2023; Mondelēz shareholder proxy materials, 2022
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Confirms existing

#### SEC Accounting Fraud — $62M Settlement
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2019–2021
- Details: The SEC charged Kraft Heinz with a multi-year expense management accounting scheme spanning Q4 2015 through end of 2018. The scheme involved recognizing unearned supplier discounts and maintaining false supplier contracts, improperly reducing cost of goods sold by $208 million across approximately 300 transactions. Kraft restated its financials in June 2019, wrote down Kraft and Oscar Mayer brands by $15 billion, reported a $12.6 billion Q4 2018 loss, and cut its dividend 36%. In September 2021, Kraft Heinz agreed to pay $62 million to settle the charges. Former COO Eduardo Pelleissone and former CPO Klaus Hofmann were separately charged. This is a major governance failure not currently in the DB.
- Source: CBS News; SEC.gov press release, September 2021; CNN Business, September 2021
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Kraft Singles American Cheese Recall
- Status: Adjudicated (voluntary recall complete)
- Date: 2023
- Details: Kraft Heinz recalled approximately 83,800 cases of individually-wrapped Kraft Singles American processed cheese slices after a temporary issue with a wrapping machine caused potential packaging defects. The recall was Class II (unlikely to cause adverse health consequences but a violation of FDA regulations). No reported illnesses.
- Source: FDA.gov; Bloomberg Law, 2023
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: New

#### Oscar Mayer Turkey Bacon — Listeria Recall and Class Action
- Status: Pending (class action filed)

Following the recall, a class action was filed in Florida federal court (filed August 2023) alleging Kraft Heinz failed to disclose that the products may be contaminated with Listeria monocytogenes, violating state and federal consumer laws. A separate 2025 Listeria-related Oscar Mayer recall affecting turkey bacon was also reported.
- Source: Fox Business; Law360; Top Class Actions, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Kraft Mac & Cheese "No Artificial Preservatives" Class Action

- Date: 2022–2023
- Details: A proposed class action alleges Kraft Heinz falsely advertised iconic Kraft Macaroni & Cheese as free from artificial preservatives. U.S. District Judge Mary Rowland ruled that plaintiffs from Illinois, California, and New York had sufficiently alleged that Kraft Mac & Cheese contains synthetic citric acid and sodium phosphates, both manufactured through synthetic industrial processes. Case allowed to proceed.

- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

- Status: Alleged / Public Record
- Date: 2022

#### Cocoa Child Labor — Class Action Lawsuit and Ongoing CBS Investigation
- Status: Pending (active lawsuit; remanded 2025)
- Date: 2023–present
- Details: In November 2023, International Rights Advocates filed a class action in the Superior Court of D.C. targeting Mars, Cargill, and Mondelēz over child labor in Ghana cocoa supply chains. A CBS Evening News investigation (November 2023) documented children as young as five harvesting cocoa for Ghana farms supplying Mars. In March 2025, a U.S. District Court judge ruled the case was improperly removed to federal court and remanded it to D.C. Superior Court, where it remains active. Mars pledged to "urgently investigate" but has made no public admissions. The ILO identifies Ivory Coast and Ghana cocoa production as involving "the worst forms of child labor."
- Source: CNBC, November 2023; LegalScoops.com, 2023; Duane Morris Blog, March 2025; Mars.com response statement; Supply Chain Brain, 2023
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Confirms existing (new litigation detail, 2025 pledge deadline now passed)

#### Kellanova Acquisition — $35.9B Consolidation
- Status: Public Record (completed)
- Date: 2024–2025
- Details: Mars completed its $35.9 billion acquisition of Kellanova (formerly Kellogg Company) on December 11, 2025, after FTC clearance on June 26, 2025.
