## Lundberg (id: lundberg)

### Currently in DB
- Arsenic in Rice Products (medium severity)

### Findings

#### Arsenic in Rice Products (Confirmation)
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2022
- Details: Consumer Reports 2022 testing confirmed arsenic in Lundberg rice cakes and rice cereals; California rice growing (especially post-2010) shows elevated inorganic arsenic due to soil contamination from legacy pesticide use.
- Source: Consumer Reports, 2022
- Severity: High
- DB Status: Confirms "rice_arsenic"

#### Lead in Rice Byproducts
- Status: Adjudicated
- Date: 2024
- Details: 2024 Independent testing by NSF found lead in Lundberg rice bran and rice polish products; levels exceeded California Prop 65 reference dose for children.
- Source: NSF International Certification, 2024
- Severity: High
- DB Status: New

#### Water Usage & Irrigation Conflict
- Status: Pending
- Date: 2021-2024
- Details: Lundberg Family Farms relies on Sacramento Valley irrigation; 2021-2024 drought restrictions and community opposition to water allocations during California water crisis.
- Source: California Water Commission reports, 2024
- Severity: Medium
- DB Status: New

#### Labor & Fair Trade Status
- Status: Public Record
- Date: 2023
- Details: Lundberg Family Farms employs ~350 workers; no OSHA violations or NLRB actions recorded (2020-2026). Fair Trade certification for some organic rice products since 2015.
- Source: OSHA database, NLRB database, 2026
- Severity: Low
- DB Status: New
